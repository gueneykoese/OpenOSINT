## Identity

Mourinho returns (appointed 11 June 2026, contract to 2029) after a trophyless 2025/26 in which Madrid finished second to Barcelona and exited the Champions League at the quarter-final stage. Expect a more structured, transition-based side built around Mbappe's and Vinicius' pace, a deeper double pivot and a priority on defensive solidity rather than the positional game attempted the previous season.

Principles: galactico/star-driven recruitment, member-owned, president-led decision making (florentino perez), champions league as the defining objective, develop-and-sell from castilla for accounting profit, no agent-fee culture on marquee deals

Character: Socio-owned institution with a powerful president; the most demanding media environment in Europe (Marca/AS daily cycle); Bernabeu whistles stars quickly; historic obligation to win the Champions League; recently renovated stadium as a revenue engine.

## Strengths

- Elite match-winners in their prime: Mbappe (27, 25 La Liga goals, top scorer 2025/26), Vinicius (26), Bellingham (23), Valverde (28)
- Young defensive spine already bought: Huijsen (21), Carreras (23), Asencio (23) plus Alexander-Arnold (27)
- Institutional Champions League pedigree and a Bernabeu revenue base that supports any transfer or wage
- Mourinho brings defensive structure and big-game pragmatism that the 2025/26 side lacked (second in La Liga, QF in Europe)

## Weaknesses

- Trophyless 2025/26 and a second consecutive title lost to Barcelona; coaching reset means a new system in September
- Ageing back-ups: Courtois 34, Carvajal 34, Alaba 34, Rudiger 33, Mendy 31 - short-term injury exposure
- No natural defensive-midfield anchor beyond Tchouameni; no like-for-like Kroos/Modric passer since 2024-25
- Summer 2026 window activity could not be verified - if arrivals were minimal, the squad is the same one that finished second
- Left-wing/striker overlap (Mbappe, Vinicius, Rodrygo) leaves a thin true No.9 alternative and a right-wing question
