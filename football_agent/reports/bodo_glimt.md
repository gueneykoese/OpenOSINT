## Identity

Knutsen's Bodø/Glimt have played the same relational 4-3-3 since 2018: high defensive line, counter-pressing, short passing through the thirds and overloads around the wingers and attacking full-backs. The artificial pitch and Arctic conditions at Aspmyra are a deliberate home advantage. In 2025/26 they beat Atlético away, knocked out Inter 5-2 on aggregate in the knockout play-off and led Sporting 3-0 before losing 3-5 on aggregate in the round of 16. In August 2026 qualifying the XI was Haikin; Sjøvold, Bjørtuft, Aleesami, Bjørkan; Auklend, Berg, Fet; Blomberg, Helmersen, Hauge - with 80% possession and 21-3 shots in the 3-0 over NEC.

Principles: one system, many years, develop norwegian/scandinavian talent and sell to bigger leagues, mental-performance coaching culture, club-wide continuity of staff

Character: Small-town club (Bodø ~50k inhabitants) with community ownership feel; extraordinary rise from second tier (2017) to Champions League round of 16; low external pressure, high internal standards; national media darling.

## Strengths

- Continuity: Knutsen (since 2018, contracted to 2029) and an unchanged 4-3-3 give a level of tactical automatism few Pot 3 sides have.
- Proven Champions League pedigree: 2025/26 round of 16 (wins over Atlético away and Inter 5-2 agg.), then a dominant 2026 qualifying run (USG 6-5, NEC 6-1 agg.).
- Aspmyra artificial pitch plus Arctic winter fixtures are a documented home advantage (home opponents this season include Dortmund and Atlético).
- 70 Eliteserien points (22-4-4) in 2025, one behind champions Viking, and the 2025/26 Norwegian Cup won on penalties v Brann.

## Weaknesses

- Striker depth: top scorer Kasper Høgh (13 league goals in 2025) sold to Celtic and Bro Hansen to PSV with no replacement signed; Helmersen is the only established No. 9.
- Injuries to Gundersen (thigh) and Evjen (knee) at the start of the European campaign; Aleesami (35) is deputising in central defence.
- Squad depth for a season running through the Norwegian off-season (Jan 2027 fixtures) is historically thin.
- Lost the 2025 Eliteserien title on the final day by one point; 5-0 collapse in Lisbon shows vulnerability under sustained pressure.
- Away form against elite pressing sides is weaker than the home record.
