## Identity

Under Luis Enrique PSG play a possession-heavy 4-3-3 with an aggressive counter-press, technical midfield (Vitinha, Joao Neves, Fabian Ruiz) and interchanging forwards rather than a fixed striker. The side that won back-to-back Champions Leagues (2024/25, 2025/26) is built on collective pressing and young, mobile attackers; the 2026 signing of Ferran Torres and Akliouche continues the preference for versatile forwards over a classic target man.

Principles: post-galactico collective project (since 2023), young french/european talent with resale value, sell surplus attackers at premium fees (ramos, lee, kolo muani, barcola in 2026), coach-led sporting model (luis enrique + luis campos)

Character: State-linked ownership with effectively unlimited backing but now operating under UEFA squad-cost rules; enormous media pressure in Paris; Parc des Princes ultras (CUP) vocal; club has pivoted from star signings to a collective identity and is now a reigning double European champion.

## Strengths

- Reigning back-to-back European champions with the same coach and core (Vitinha, Neves, Hakimi, Mendes, Marquinhos, Dembele, Doue, Kvaratskhelia) - continuity is rare at this level
- Best attack (74) and best defence (29 conceded) in Ligue 1 2025/26; league title with a game to spare for a fifth straight season
- Elite young ball-playing midfield (Vitinha 26, Neves 21, Zaire-Emery 20, Mayulu 20) suited to Luis Enrique's positional game
- Squad-cost discipline: roughly EUR 250m+ raised from sales of surplus attackers in 2026, funding reinvestment without FFP exposure

## Weaknesses

- Loss of Barcola (top league scorer, 11) and of three back-up strikers (Ramos, Kolo Muani) thins the goal supply; Ferran Torres is a versatile forward rather than a proven 25-goal No.9
- Marquinhos (32) and Lucas Hernandez (30) are ageing at CB; depth behind Pacho/Zabarnyi is young (Beraldo 22)
- Recurring muscle injuries for Dembele and full-back absences (Mendes/Hakimi) in 2025/26 exposed a thin back-up at full-back - the 33-year-old Digne is a short-term fix
- Domestic complacency risk: cup exit to Paris FC and Trophee des Champions defeat to Lens in Aug 2026 show vulnerability in one-off games
