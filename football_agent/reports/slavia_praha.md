## Identity

Trpišovský (appointed 22 December 2017, contract to 2029 with a further three-year option; the longest-serving coach in Chance Liga history) runs a high-intensity, physically aggressive team. Domestically Slavia typically use a 3-4-3; in the 2025/26 Champions League they opened in a 4-2-3-1 against Bodø/Glimt but switched to a more cautious 3-4-2-1 away at Inter and Atalanta. The 2025/26 league record (24-8-3, 74-31, 80 points) reflects a side that dominates possession and territory at home but struggled to convert that into UCL points (34th of 36).

Principles: coach continuity (same head coach since 2017), africa and balkans scouting network (ogbu, diakité, dorley, sanyang, mbodji, n'guessan, ayaosi), buy young, sell to western europe (pech to young boys, zafeiris/prekop/zmrzlý sales), billionaire-owner stability rather than member governance, target the ucl league phase every season

Character: 100% owned since 2018 by Czech billionaire Pavel Tykač (via Fulleren), with Jaroslav Tvrdík as long-time board chairman. Traditional Prague giant with an intense rivalry with Sparta; Fortuna Arena (Eden) crowd is the most vocal in the country. Pressure is high but governance is stable and patient by regional standards. Czech media environment is intense but smaller-scale than Istanbul.

## Strengths

- Most stable coaching setup in the draw: Trpišovský since Dec 2017, contract to 2029, domestic double-digit title margins (80 pts, only 3 defeats).
- Physical, aerially dominant spine (Chorý 17 league goals, Ogbu, Zima, Holeš) that thrives on set pieces.
- Deep, young African-recruited squad gives rotation and resale value; eight summer additions without losing key starters (Chorý, Douděra retained).
- Second consecutive league phase: the group learned from the 2025/26 campaign (2 points after 4 games, 34th overall).
- Loud home ground (Eden) and a favourable-looking home slate (Arsenal, Aston Villa, Villarreal, Lens) versus the tough away trips.

## Weaknesses

- Finished 34th of 36 in the 2025/26 league phase; conceded heavily against elite attacks (Inter, Arsenal).
- Limited pace at the back when pressed high; the switch to a 3-4-2-1 away in Europe shows the coach's own caution.
- Creativity concentrated in Provod; no verified replacement creator arrived after Pech and Zafeiris sales.
- Domestic league tempo is well below UCL level, so match sharpness for Tuesday nights is an annual problem.
- 2025/26 assist leader and several squad ages/contracts could not be verified.
