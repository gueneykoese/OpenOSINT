## Identity

Under Okan Buruk (appointed 23 June 2022) Galatasaray mostly play a 4-2-3-1 that becomes a 4-4-2 block out of possession, with an aggressive pressing game Turkish media label 'hell press'. The attack is built around a reference striker (Osimhen, 15 league / 22 all-competition goals in 2025/26) fed by direct wingers (Barış Alper Yılmaz, 12 league assists; Sané; Yunus Akgün) and a ball-playing double pivot (Torreira, Gündoğan, Sara). In 2025/26 they scored 77 and conceded 30 in 34 league games and reached the UCL round of 16, where they lost to Juventus.

Principles: domestic dominance first (four consecutive titles 2022/23-2025/26), star-driven recruitment of established internationals (osimhen, sané, gündoğan, torreira), big-fee gambles on emerging talent (batrakov eur 30m at 21, nhaga eur 6.5m), 10+4 foreign-player rule shapes squad building, coach continuity: same head coach since 2022

Character: Member-owned association (Galatasaray SK) with football run through the listed company Galatasaray Sportif A.Ş.; one of Turkey's biggest fanbases and the most decorated club domestically. Very high pressure and a saturated Istanbul media environment, but comparatively stable governance versus rivals: President Dursun Özbek and coach Okan Buruk have been in place through the four-title run.

## Strengths

- Serial domestic winners: four straight titles, 77 points and +47 goal difference in 2025/26 under an unchanged coach.
- Elite No.9 in Osimhen (22 goals all competitions 2025/26) plus a top-level creator in Barış Alper Yılmaz (12 league assists).
- Experienced international midfield (Gündoğan, Torreira, Sara, Lemina) comfortable in European knockout football (UCL last 16 in 2025/26).
- Squad depth in wide areas (Sané, Yunus Akgün, Sallai, Barış Alper) allows rotation across a 34-game league plus eight UCL games.
- Financial firepower to pay EUR 30m for a 21-year-old (Batrakov) while keeping the core.

## Weaknesses

- Striker depth: Icardi left at contract end and only Osimhen is a verified out-and-out No.9 for 2026/27.
- Centre-back options thinned after Nelsson's exit; Kaan Ayhan is the main cover behind Sánchez and Bardakcı.
- Ageing spine (Gündoğan, Torreira, Sánchez all 30+) may struggle with the Tuesday-Saturday rhythm.
- Finances under UEFA monitoring (publicly acknowledged by the president) limit further spending.
- Draw is hard: Barcelona, PSG, Aston Villa and Sporting are all above them on coefficient.
