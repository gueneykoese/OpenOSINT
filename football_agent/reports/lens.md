## Strengths

- Best defence in Ligue 1 2025/26 (35 GA in 34) - structured, hard to break down.
- Winning habit: 8 straight league wins, runners-up, first Coupe de France title.
- Thauvin as an elite Ligue 1 creator (14 G / 10 A) still in the squad.
- Bollaert atmosphere is a genuine home advantage for the four home UCL games.
- Financially healthy after EUR 48m Sangaré sale.

## Weaknesses

- Coach change in June 2026 (Sage to Premier League, Toppmöller in): new principles must be installed while playing UCL for the first time since 2023/24.
- Sangaré's departure removes the midfield's ball-winner and engine; Cuisance/Titraoui are different profiles.
- Thomasson and Saïd out - loss of experienced depth in the front line.
- Thauvin is 33 and Hazard 33: the creative unit is ageing.
