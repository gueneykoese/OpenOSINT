## Identity

Dambrauskas (appointed June 2025 on a three-year deal) won the domestic double in his first season - the league sealed with four rounds to spare, the cup 2-1 against Zira - and then navigated four UCL qualifying rounds. The team is built around the goals of Joy-Lance Mickels (19 league / 28 all-competition goals in 2025/26, contract extended by two seasons) with quick wide play; the 6-4 aggregate vs Hapoel Be'er Sheva shows an attack-first, defensively open profile. Formation not verifiable this session.

Principles: young club (founded 2017) built to challenge qarabağ, imports from african, portuguese, balkan and post-soviet markets (mccarthy, almeida, mbina, simić, nwachukwu, pokatilov, rahmonaliev), loan-to-buy conversions (simić, mbina) and loans from bigger clubs (lachaab from lille, du queiroz from zenit), retain the domestic core (mutallimov extended to 2028)

Character: Baku club founded 2017, plays at the Bank Respublika Arena in Masazır; small organic fan base, low domestic media pressure, but the first UCL league-phase appearance for a non-Qarabağ Azerbaijani club brings national attention.

## Strengths

- Domestic double champions with a coach who has now won titles in Lithuania, Bulgaria and Azerbaijan.
- Proven qualifier toughness: four rounds cleared, including the 6-4 aggregate play-off.
- Mickels is a bona fide goal source (28 in all comps 2025/26) and has been re-signed for two more seasons.
- Squad strengthened rather than sold: two loanees made permanent, five external additions incl. a Kazakhstan international GK (Pokatilov) and a Zenit loanee (Du Queiroz), key domestic midfielder Mutallimov retained.

## Weaknesses

- Open defence: conceded four over two play-off legs; will face Barcelona, Arsenal, Dortmund and Napoli-level attacks.
- Lowest-ranked league in the draw - the step in quality and intensity is the largest of any Pot-4 club.
- Heavy dependence on one scorer (Mickels); no verified second striker of comparable output.
- Home/away split of the draw is unkind: the three most winnable-looking ties (Viking, Villarreal, Slavia) include two away trips.
