## Identity

Under Van Persie (2025/26) Feyenoord played an aggressive, front-foot 4-3-3 built around striker Ayase Ueda, who won the Eredivisie golden boot with 25 league goals; the side scored 70 but conceded 44 in 34 games, i.e. attack-heavy and defensively porous. Van Bronckhorst, appointed 17 June 2026 on a two-year deal, has started 2026/27 in a 4-3-3 (Ernst; Read, St. Juste, Watanabe/Kraaijeveld, Mármol; Vanhoutte, Valente, Zechiël; Hadj Moussa, Ueda, Diarra) - Ueda was then sold to Lille on deadline day, leaving Ferri/Tengstedt as the No. 9 options.

Principles: develop-and-sell (eredivisie business model), strong academy pathway (varkenoord), recruit undervalued players from smaller leagues, club-icon coaching appointments

Character: Working-class Rotterdam club with one of the most intense fan cultures in the Netherlands (De Kuip); expectation is to compete with PSV and Ajax every year; heavy media scrutiny nationally; second-best resourced club in the league.

## Strengths

- Elite Eredivisie goal threat in 2025/26: 70 team goals, second only to champions PSV (but golden-boot winner Ueda has now been sold).
- Home advantage at De Kuip, historically one of the toughest away trips in Dutch/European football.
- Stable, experienced coach who knows the club (Van Bronckhorst won the KNVB Cup 2016 and Eredivisie 2017 in his first spell) and has fielded a settled 4-3-3 from matchday 1.
- Recruitment record of turning modest fees into big sales (Ueda, Ahmedhodzic, Timber buy-back clause) gives financial headroom; seven new signings and a leaner squad.

## Weaknesses

- 44 goals conceded in 34 league games (1.29/game) is too many for a title challenger and will be exposed against Pot 1-2 attacks (Barcelona, Inter).
- Europa League league-phase exit (29th, 6 pts) and an early KNVB Cup exit (round 2) point to inconsistency outside the league.
- Ueda (25 league goals) sold on deadline day with no like-for-like replacement: Ferri (unproven) and Tengstedt are the No. 9 options.
- Centre-back churn: Ahmedhodzic and Trauner gone, Watanabe injured in pre-season; St. Juste (29) and Kraaijeveld started the opener.
- Diarra (LW) scored 0 in 7 Eredivisie apps last season yet started the first games; wide depth relies on the 26-year-old free agent Nelson.
