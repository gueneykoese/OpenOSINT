## Identity

Kompany's Bayern press extremely high with an aggressive back line and dominate territory; 122 Bundesliga goals in 2025/26 (a league record) with Harry Kane (36) as the reference point and Olise/Diaz/Musiala/Gnabry supplying from wide and the half-spaces. Kimmich and Laimer/Bischof provide the double pivot; the trade-off is exposure to transitions (Bayern still conceded ~40 despite the +82 difference).

Principles: 'mia san mia' - win every domestic title, financially self-sustaining (member-majority ownership), sign established bundesliga-proven talent plus selective marquee buys, strong preference for german internationals in the core, campus youth pathway (bischof, kiala, urbig)

Character: Member-controlled club with three strategic corporate minority shareholders; conservative finances (no debt), enormous domestic expectation, a famously blunt board/media culture (Hoeness, Rummenigge, Eberl, Freund) that scrutinises coaches publicly.

## Strengths

- Record-breaking attack: 122 league goals and a +82 difference in 2025/26; Kane 36 league goals
- Only 1-2 league defeats all season; domestic double (Bundesliga + DFB-Pokal) and a UCL semi-final under Kompany
- Settled coach in his third season with a clear high-press identity and a core of German internationals (Tah, Kimmich, Musiala, Goretzka)
- Wide talent in its prime: Olise (24), Diaz (29), Musiala (23) plus Saibari added

## Weaknesses

- Age at key spots: Neuer 40 in goal, Kane 33 as the sole elite No.9, Kimmich/Goretzka/Gnabry all 31
- Palhinha sold with no like-for-like defensive-midfield ball-winner brought in; Bischof/Pavlovic are 21-22
- Very high line concedes chances in transition - ~40 league goals against is high for an 86-point champion; PSG exploited it in the semi-final
- Right-back is a converted midfielder (Laimer) with Boey marginalised and Stanisic multi-purpose; thin specialist depth
