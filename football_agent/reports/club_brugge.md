## Identity

Leko's first Club Brugge side (2017-19) was known for a dominant, attacking 3-5-2 built around Vanaken; in his second spell (from Dec 2025) he inherited Hayen's title-chasing squad and delivered the 20th championship via the play-offs, but has publicly criticised his team for wanting to 'do it too nicely' and demanded harder work (Sporza, Aug 2026). The 2025/26 attack ran through Tzolis (22 goals, 29 assists in 52 games) and Tresoldi (23 goals); with Tzolis, Stankovic and Onyedika sold, the 2026/27 team is being re-shaped around Vanaken, Potts, Tresoldi and young wide players.

Principles: buy-to-sell talent model with record exits (tzolis eur 40m to arsenal - jpl record; stankovic, onyedika: the three sales brought in eur 70m+), reinvest in young, undervalued profiles from switzerland, scandinavia and england (vasovic, tsawa, lee, potts, angulo), majority-owner-driven long-term project incl. a new 40,116-seat stadium (olympia site, target 2028-29), club nxt reserve team as a pathway (angulo placed there), annual ucl participation as a financial pillar (5th league-phase campaign in a row)

Character: Belgium's dominant club of the last decade (titles 2016, 2018, 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024, 2026) with a passionate, demanding fanbase at the ageing Jan Breydel Stadium and a strong regional identity in West Flanders. Media pressure is moderate compared with the big-five leagues, but expectations are the title every season plus UCL knockout football; the 2025/26 3-3 home draw with Atletico showed the atmosphere the ground can generate.

## Strengths

- Serial domestic winners (20th title in 2026) with five consecutive UCL league-phase campaigns and a 2025/26 knockout appearance.
- Proven scorer retained: Tresoldi (19 league / 23 total goals in 2025/26, contract to 2029) despite Barcelona/Premier League interest.
- Strong selling record funds continual reinvestment: Tzolis EUR 40m (JPL record), Stankovic and Onyedika together EUR 70m+.
- Experienced spine in Vanaken, Mechele and new GK Sommer around young full-backs Seys and Sabbe.
- Stable, well-capitalised private ownership with a new stadium approved.

## Weaknesses

- Creative output gutted: Tzolis contributed 22 goals and 29 assists in 2025/26 and has not been directly replaced.
- Both first-choice holding midfielders (Onyedika, Stankovic) sold in one window; Potts and 19-year-old Tsawa step in.
- Centre-back turnover (Ordonez and Romero out, Lee in) ahead of ties with Liverpool, Inter and Napoli.
- Regular-season defence was porous for a champion (36 conceded in 30 games) and the side conceded 7 in two legs to Atletico.
- Coach's tactical model for 2026/27 not yet settled; Leko publicly critical of the squad's mentality in Aug 2026.
