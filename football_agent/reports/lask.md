## Identity

Kühbauer took over on 9 Oct 2025 with LASK second-bottom and won the double; the season was defined by comebacks (0-4 down vs Celtic, extra-time cup final win). Data from the title run - 56 scored, 42 conceded in 32 games - shows an attack-first, transition team rather than a control team.

Principles: develop-and-sell (cisse to aston villa, previous sales to premier league), linz regional identity, raiffeisen arena as commercial engine, pragmatic coaching hires

Character: Member-association club run by a private board (president Siegmund Gruber) with strong regional backing in Upper Austria; the 2025/26 double ended a 61-year trophy drought, so expectations and media attention are at an all-time high.

## Strengths

- Momentum and mentality: double winners who came from second-bottom in October and overturned 0-4 vs Celtic - a resilient dressing room.
- Coach continuity (Kühbauer extended to 2029 in Aug 2026) with the whole staff retained.
- Extra-time/late-game goal threat (Bello, Adeniran in cup final; Celtic comeback).
- Core of the title squad largely retained apart from Cisse and Kalajdžić.

## Weaknesses

- Lost the two most valuable players: Cisse (CB, to Aston Villa) and loan striker Kalajdžić - the No.9 role is now Adeniran/Maksimov, unproven at UCL level.
- Defence conceded 42 in 32 league games - worst of the top three; will be exposed by Liverpool/Real/Porto.
- Only 39 points (halved Meistergruppe system) - title margin over Sturm was 2 points; squad depth for UCL + domestic double defence is thin.
- No European football in 2025/26, so zero recent continental experience in the squad.
