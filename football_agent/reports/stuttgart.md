## Identity

Sebastian Hoeneß took over in April 2023 with the club 18th, survived via the relegation play-off, finished runner-up in 2023/24 and has now delivered a DFB-Pokal win (2025), a Pokal final (2026) and two UCL qualifications. His Stuttgart prioritise ball dominance (averaging over 57% possession) with an intense, centrally focused press out of a 4-2-3-1 that morphs into 4-4-2/4-2-2-2. In 2025/26 they had the Bundesliga's second-best attack, a record 41 away goals, and a home record of 17-3-5 across all competitions; Deniz Undav scored 19 league goals (second only to Harry Kane) and 25 in all competitions.

Principles: coach continuity and early contract extensions (hoeneß to 2028), develop-and-sell talent pathway (woltemade 2025, chema andrés eur 25m to brighton 2026, millot 2025), reinvest in bundesliga-proven 22-26-year-olds (pejčinović eur 25m, sauer eur 13m, el khannouss obligation eur 18m), regional identity: mercedes-benz and porsche as anchor investors, 50+1 member-controlled ag with strong supervisory-board oversight

Character: Members' club (VfB Stuttgart 1893 e.V.) that spun off the professional team into VfB Stuttgart 1893 AG in 2017; the e.V. holds the majority under the 50+1 rule, with Mercedes-Benz and Porsche as minority strategic investors. Passionate Swabian fanbase (MHP Arena ~60,000), a demanding regional press (Stuttgarter Zeitung, kicker) but a board (CEO Alexander Wehrle, sporting director Fabian Wohlgemuth) that has backed the coach through dips.

## Strengths

- Elite Bundesliga attack: second-best offence in 2025/26, record 41 away goals, Undav 19 league / 25 total goals and now tied to 2029.
- Tactical clarity and continuity under Hoeneß (since April 2023, contract to 2028): possession-dominant with a coordinated central press.
- Strong home form (17 wins, 3 draws, 5 defeats in all home games 2025/26) in a 60,000 arena; UCL home slate of Atlético, Brugge, Lille, Viking is manageable.
- Smart squad refresh: Pejčinović, Sauer, El Khannouss and Prömel added while selling Chema Andrés for EUR 25m.
- Recent European experience: Europa League last 16 in 2025/26 and a UCL league phase in 2024/25.

## Weaknesses

- Goalkeeping is the big gamble: Nübel returned to Bayern and 20-year-old Dennis Seimen (one 2. Bundesliga season) is the declared No.1 for a UCL campaign.
- European knockout record is poor - eliminated by Porto 1-4 on aggregate and out of the 2024/25 UCL league phase.
- Sold their best young defensive midfielder (Chema Andrés); Stiller/Karazor now lack a natural successor.
- Zagadou's long injury history and a thin verified centre-back list (Chabot, Zagadou) behind a high defensive line.
- Squad list for 2026/27 only partially verifiable in-session (15 names) - depth players not confirmed.
