## Identity

Under İsmail Kartal (appointed June 2026 on a one-year deal, his fourth spell) Turkish media report a 4-3-3 as the main plan with 4-2-3-1 as the alternative used in some pre-season games, and Kartal publicly demanded a 'pressing' game before the season opener at Gençlerbirliği. Squad construction points to a senior, experienced core (Ederson, Škriniar, Aké, Guendouzi, Asensio, Lukaku, Greenwood) built to win domestic duels and compete in Europe rather than a young transition team.

Principles: title-now imperative (no league title since 2013/14), experienced-star recruitment, domestic-legend coaching appointments, heavy reliance on loan returns and squad churn, 10+4 foreign-player rule shapes squad building

Character: Member-owned association with one of Turkey's largest fanbases; extremely high pressure and volatile governance — three presidents in twelve months (Ali Koç -> Sadettin Saran, elected 21 Sep 2025 -> Aziz Yıldırım, elected 7 Jun 2026 with 17,245 votes vs 9,927 for Hakan Safi, on record turnout of 27,000+). Coaches and sporting directors are changed quickly: Domenico Tedesco and sporting director Devin Özek were dismissed on 27 Apr 2026, a day after a 0-3 derby defeat at Galatasaray ended the title race. Intense Istanbul media environment.

## Strengths

- Proven European-level spine added in one window: Ederson (GK), Aké and Škriniar (CB), Guendouzi (CM), Asensio, Greenwood and Lukaku — all with Champions League experience.
- Momentum: qualified for the UCL league phase for the first time since 2008/09 by winning 2-1 away at Lyon after a 1-1 home draw; Greenwood scored in the first leg.
- Strong domestic base: 2nd in the 2025/26 Süper Lig, Turkish Super Cup 2025 winner, and a settled, experienced Turkish coach who knows the club (Kartal's 4th stint).
- Depth in wide/attacking midfield after loan returns (Ünder, İrfan Can Kahveci) and the Greenwood signing gives multiple options for a 4-3-3.

## Weaknesses

- Governance volatility: three presidents in twelve months and coach/sporting-director dismissals in April 2026; short-term decision horizon (president and coach both on one-year terms).
- Ageing spine: Ederson, Lukaku and Fred are 33, Semedo and Muriqi 32, Škriniar and Aké 31 — high injury and decline risk over a 34-game league plus UCL schedule.
- Loss of top scorer: Talisca (19 league / 27 total goals in 2025/26) left on contract expiry; goal responsibility shifts to 33-year-old Lukaku.
- 10+4 foreign-player rule forced a planned clear-out of foreigners (Oosterwolde, Brown, Semedo, Fred, Diego Carlos reported for sale/loan in July 2026), disrupting continuity at full-back and defensive midfield.
- Europe: eliminated in the UCL play-off in Aug 2025 and in the Europa League knockout play-off (2-4 agg. v Nottingham Forest) — no recent record of going deep in Europe.
