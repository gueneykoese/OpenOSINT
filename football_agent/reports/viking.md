## Identity

Viking under the Aarsheim/Jensen duo (sixth season in 2026) are an attack-minded team that won the 2025 title with a seven-match winning finish and a 5-1 final-day win; in Europe they beat Koper 12-3 on aggregate and Dinamo Zagreb 5-3. The team prioritises goals over control and relies on striker Peter Christiansen (14 league goals in 2025).

Principles: member-owned club with academy pathway, sell-on model to bigger scandinavian/european leagues, coaching continuity (same duo since 2021)

Character: Norway's oldest top-flight fixture with a strong Stavanger regional identity; first league title in 34 years (2025) has created a rare wave of local euphoria; calendar-year season means the UCL league phase spans the Norwegian off-season.

## Strengths

- Prolific attack and momentum: 12-3 and 5-3 aggregate wins in Europe in successive seasons; 71-point title season.
- Rare coaching stability (same duo since 2021) with a clear attacking identity.
- Home crowd and Nordic winter conditions at SR-Bank Arena are a real edge in December/January UCL rounds.
- Champions-League revenue (first ever) transforms a modest budget.

## Weaknesses

- Defensive numbers unknown but the qualifier scorelines (5-3, 3-1 loss to Başakşehir) point to a porous back line against elite attacks.
- Norwegian calendar: league season ends late Nov, so UCL MD6-8 (Dec-Jan) fall in the off-season - fitness and rhythm risk.
- Very limited squad-list verification for 2026/27 - dependence on a small core (Christiansen, Tripić, Svendsen).
- Goalkeeper churn (four GKs moved in 2026) suggests instability in a key position.
