## Identity

Under Genesio (2024-26) Lille combined a compact mid-block with quick vertical transitions and set-piece efficiency, finishing 3rd in Ligue 1 (61 pts, 18-7-9, with 10 away wins and a league-best 23 goals after the 75th minute) and reaching the Europa League round of 16. Davide Ancelotti (appointed 1 June 2026) inherits a squad reshaped by 21 departures; he opened Ligue 1 with a 2-0 win at Angers (Giroud, Tiago Santos) before the deadline-day sale of Fernández-Pardo and arrival of Ueda.

Principles: trading model: buy young, develop, sell high, data-informed scouting (létang era), financial discipline after 2020 debt crisis, mix of veterans (giroud) and prospects

Character: Provincial club competing with far smaller budget than PSG; supporters accept squad churn as long as the club stays in Europe; pressure moderate; president Olivier Létang is the dominant public figure.

## Strengths

- Proven ability to over-perform budget: 3rd in Ligue 1 (61 pts, 18-7-9) and a Europa League last-16 run in 2025/26; league-best 23 goals after the 75th minute.
- Exceptionally healthy finances after a +EUR 133m summer (Bouaddi ~EUR 95m, Fernández-Pardo ~EUR 65m), giving flexibility in January.
- A proven Eredivisie golden-boot striker (Ueda, 25 goals) added to Giroud's experience; Giroud scored in the opening 2-0 win at Angers.
- Stable executive leadership (Létang) with clear recruitment identity; long contracts on the core (Özer, Haraldsson, Sahraoui, Igamane to 2029-30).

## Weaknesses

- 21 departures in one window: squad cohesion and depth are unproven at Champions League level; Bouaddi and Fernández-Pardo were two of the best young players.
- Rookie head coach: Davide Ancelotti has no prior head-coaching record in a top-five league.
- Goal threat thin before Ueda: top scorer Giroud managed only 7 league goals at age 39; Igamane and Nianzou missed the season opener.
- Only EUR 42m reinvested; Önal (EUR 12m) and Kjaergaard (EUR 8m) must replace Bouaddi's midfield output immediately.
- Pot 3 draw is tough: Bayern at home, Arsenal, Stuttgart and Roma away, plus the Aspmyra artificial pitch.
