## Identity

Under Rui Borges, Sporting build patiently with one midfielder (formerly Hjulmand) dropping between the centre-backs and the right-back tucking inside, then attack at volume - 89 league goals (best in Portugal) in 2025/26 and 2.2 goals per game across his first 89 matches. Borges has never coached a back-three side by preference and has gradually moved from Amorim's 3-4-3 to his own back-four model, prioritising more defensive balance than his predecessor. The 2026/27 side is a rebuilt, very young group (average age around 21) after record sales.

Principles: develop-and-sell at record prices (quenda eur 50m, trincao ~eur 44m, hjulmand eur 40m+5m, diomande eur 40m) with huge release clauses on new signings (eur 80m on altimira, ba, kaique), reinvest sales into young, high-upside profiles (10 signings for 2026/27, eur 116m+ spent), coach continuity: rui borges contracted to 2028, academy pathway (alcochete) - rafael nel handed the no. 9, flavio goncalves and joao simoes in the senior group, win the title while competing in the ucl after two league titles (2024, 2025) and a runner-up finish

Character: One of Portugal's 'big three', with a large, passionate fanbase at the Estadio Jose Alvalade and heavy daily coverage from A Bola, Record and O Jogo. High pressure after losing the title to Porto and the Taca de Portugal final to second-tier Torreense in May 2026; fans are watching a wholesale squad rebuild after the biggest sales window in club history.

## Strengths

- Most prolific attack in Portugal in 2025/26 (89 league goals; 51 at home) and only two league defeats, none away (12-5-0).
- Luis Suarez: 38 goals in all competitions in 2025/26 including 5 in 12 UCL games - a proven UCL-level No. 9 retained despite the sales.
- Deep UCL experience as a club: quarter-finalists in 2025/26 (eliminated 0-1 on aggregate by Arsenal).
- Financial firepower from record sales (EUR 117m+ received) reinvested in ten young signings; EUR 80m clauses protect asset value.
- Coach stability: Rui Borges contracted to 2028 with a 2.2-goals-per-game record over 89 matches.

## Weaknesses

- Leadership and spine gutted in one window: captain Hjulmand, Diomande, Trincao (10 league assists), Pote and Quenda all sold.
- Very young, largely unproven group (average age ~21 per Borges) with 10 new signings to integrate while playing Barcelona, City, United and Roma.
- Defensive concessions in a chaotic 2025/26 (24 conceded, 2 home defeats) and two cup-final/quarter-final exits show a gap to elite level; Torreense final loss exposed mentality under pressure.
- Reliance on Suarez for goals: Ioannidis and 19-year-old Nel are the only alternatives after Trincao/Pote left.
- Tactical identity still in transition between Amorim's 3-4-3 and Borges's back-four.
