## Identity

Under Kovač Dortmund abandoned their old 'heavy-metal' identity for a structured 3-4-2-1: Anton, Schlotterbeck and Bensebaini in front of Kobel, wing-backs providing the width, Sabitzer/Nmecha (now Jobe Bellingham, Chukwuemeka) in the double pivot and two flexible No. 10s behind Guirassy. In 2025/26 that produced the league's best defence (34 conceded), 70 goals and 73 points — 13 more than 2024/25 — but the team was still 2nd behind a record-breaking Bayern and collapsed from 2-0 up to lose 3-4 on aggregate to Atalanta in the UCL. The 2026 window replaced experienced attackers (Brandt, Adeyemi) with teenagers (Karetsas 18, Gadou 19, Lerma 18, Prates 17) plus the Nwaneri loan.

Principles: buy-young-develop-sell: 2026 recruits karetsas, gadou, lerma, prates are all 19 or under, member-owned club with a listed kgaa, run within its own means (no oligarch/state money), bundesliga's best defensive record as the platform for a title challenge, coaching stability: kovač extended to 2027 after finishing 2nd, südtribüne/yellow wall fan culture and 81k attendances as commercial engine

Character: Member-controlled club (BV Borussia 09 e.V. holds the majority of the KGaA, which is listed on the Frankfurt Stock Exchange — Germany's only listed football club). Enormous, loyal fanbase and the 81,365-capacity Signal Iduna Park with the Yellow Wall; media pressure is high but the club is financially self-sustaining and openly positions itself as Bayern's challenger with a fraction of the budget.

## Strengths

- Best defence in the 2025/26 Bundesliga: 34 goals conceded in 34 games behind a settled Anton-Schlotterbeck-Bensebaini back three and Kobel.
- Big improvement trajectory: 73 points (+13 on 2024/25), 22-7-5, 70 scored; 2nd place and direct UCL qualification under Kovač, who is contracted to 2027.
- Reliable No. 9: Guirassy 17 Bundesliga goals (3rd in the league) plus 4 UCL and 1 cup goal; his release clause expired unexercised in July 2026 so he stays.
- Deep, youthful attacking-midfield pool: Karetsas (EUR 30m), Nwaneri (Arsenal loan), Lerma, Beier, Chukwuemeka, Jobe Bellingham — high resale value.
- Financial self-sufficiency and stable governance (member-owned, listed KGaA) with strong sales record (Adeyemi, Duranville, Campbell, Wätjen sold in 2026).

## Weaknesses

- Experience drain in attack: Brandt (11 goals all comps) and Adeyemi left; replacements are 17-19-year-olds and a 19-year-old loanee with no guarantee of immediate output.
- European fragility: threw away a 2-0 first-leg lead to lose 3-4 agg to Atalanta; earlier DFB-Pokal exit in the round of 16 to Leverkusen.
- Midfield leadership: captain Emre Can (32) is recovering from an ACL tear; Özcan and Groß gone; Sabitzer ageing — Nmecha/Bellingham must carry the pivot.
- Goalkeeper depth: Meyer is 35, Ostrzinski untested, Ramaj loaned out — Kobel has no proven deputy.
- Guirassy publicly wanted to leave in summer 2026; dependence on one striker whose commitment was questioned.
