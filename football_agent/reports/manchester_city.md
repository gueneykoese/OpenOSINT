## Identity

Maresca (appointed 29 June 2026, three-year deal) is a Guardiola disciple who at Chelsea used a 4-2-3-1 that becomes a 3-2-5 in possession, with the goalkeeper heavily involved in build-up, a left-back inverting into midfield and a slow-then-fast positional attack. The transition from Guardiola's decade should be stylistically smooth; the squad already contains the ball-playing profiles (Rodri, Reijnders, Cherki, Foden) and the elite finisher (Haaland).

Principles: city football group multi-club model and global scouting, positional-play coaching lineage (guardiola to maresca), succession planning: sign players 20-25 for future core, sell academy graduates for pure profit, sporting director-led (hugo viana since 2025)

Character: Abu Dhabi-backed ownership with vast resources but a decade of legal/regulatory scrutiny; relatively calm fan base, Etihad expansion; first post-Guardiola season creates unusual uncertainty; club also faces exits of veterans (Stones, Bernardo Silva) marking the end of an era.

## Strengths

- Second in the Premier League (78 pts, 23-9-6) - only six defeats, the best record outside champions Arsenal
- Haaland (26) remains the most reliable goal source in Europe; Donnarumma (27) an elite GK signed in 2025
- Deep pool of 21-25-year-old technicians (Reijnders, Cherki, Gvardiol, Khusanov, Ait-Nouri, Lewis, O'Reilly, Savinho, Doku) aligned to Maresca's positional model
- Stylistic continuity: Maresca's system is a direct descendant of Guardiola's, limiting adaptation cost

## Weaknesses

- First season without Guardiola since 2016; Maresca inherits a squad that finished 7 points behind Arsenal and has to re-establish authority
- Loss of dressing-room leaders Stones and Bernardo Silva (both left summer 2026) removes experience and a CB option
- Rodri (30) is irreplaceable as the single pivot - his 2024/25 ACL history makes DM depth a structural risk
- Right-back remains improvised (Lewis, Nunes, Khusanov) with no specialist starter
- Champions League 2025/26 exit round and cup results could not be verified; European form since 2024 has been below the club's standard
