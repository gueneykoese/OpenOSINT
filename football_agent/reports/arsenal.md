## Identity

Arteta's Arsenal play a 4-3-3 in possession that becomes a compact 4-4-2 without the ball, with Saka doing more defensive work than most elite wingers. Build-up is flexible: midfielders alternately drop into the back line or show ahead of it so progression can be tailored to the opponent. The 2025/26 title was built on the division's best defence (27 goals conceded, 19 clean sheets, lowest xGA in Europe's top five leagues at ~0.7 per game) rather than on volume attacking (71 goals, 66.1 xG). With Zubimendi at the base, Rice was released as a box-crashing No. 8; Guimaraes' arrival adds a second press-resistant carrier on the right of the three.

Principles: defensive solidity and set-piece efficiency first, buy established premier league / top-five-league players in their prime (konsa 28, guimaraes 28, eze 28, gyokeres 28), hale end academy pathway (saka, nwaneri, lewis-skelly, dowman), long coaching continuity under arteta since dec 2019, trim fringe wages each summer (trossard, norgaard, kiwior, vieira, jesus out in 2026)

Character: Fully owned by Kroenke Sports & Entertainment since the 2018 compulsory buy-out of minority shareholders; large global fanbase, 60k Emirates Stadium, intense London media environment. Pressure is now about converting a title-winning core into European success after the 2026 Champions League final defeat on penalties.

## Strengths

- Best defence in Europe's top five leagues in 2025/26: 27 goals conceded, 28.5 xGA (~0.7 per game), 19 clean sheets; Raya named best goalkeeper.
- Midfield depth and press resistance: Zubimendi (3,001 PL mins, 5 goals) anchoring, Rice (3,099 mins, 4G/5A) as a box-arriving No. 8, plus the GBP 75m addition of Guimaraes and Odegaard/Merino/Eze as alternatives.
- Centre-back depth after Konsa (GBP 51m+4m) and Hincapie's permanent deal: six senior CB options, allowing Arsenal to absorb Saliba's long-term absence.
- Proven big-game pedigree: Premier League champions (85 pts, 7 clear), 5-1 aggregate win over holders Real Madrid in the UCL R16, Champions League finalists.
- Coaching continuity: Arteta in post since December 2019 with a stable, well-drilled 4-3-3/4-4-2 structure and elite set-piece routines.
- Clinical No. 9: Gyokeres converted 14 PL goals from 55 shots (25.5%), the best goals-to-shots ratio among Arsenal's top scorers.

## Weaknesses

- Spread of goals is thin behind Gyokeres: only Saka and Eze (7 each) reached seven league goals, and 71 GF / 66 xG is modest for a champion; Trossard (6 assists, 5-6 goals) has left.
- Chronic availability problems: Saliba (back), Timber (groin surgery) and Eze (calf) all out at the start of 2026/27; Havertz missed most of 2025/26 after knee surgery; Odegaard had shoulder/knee issues.
- Right-back is unsettled: Timber injured, White described as 'a shadow' of his best, Mosquera/Konsa are converted CBs.
- Penalty/knockout mental scar tissue: lost the UCL final and EFL Cup final in 2025/26 (Gabriel missed the decisive penalty in Budapest).
- Ageing/prime-age recruitment (Guimaraes, Konsa, Eze, Gyokeres all 28) limits resale value and concentrates the wage bill (reported GBP 244m, 2nd in PL).
- Left-wing output: Martinelli scored 1 PL goal in 2025/26 and is in the final year of his contract; Tzolis is unproven in the Premier League.
