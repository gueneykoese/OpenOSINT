## Identity

Marcelino's 2025/26 Villarreal were a disciplined 4-4-2 counter-attacking side that finished 3rd, overtaking Atlético with a 5-1 final-day win, with Georges Mikautadze scoring 13 league goals; in Europe the same side lost seven of eight league-phase games. Iñigo Pérez (appointed 1 June 2026) arrives from Rayo Vallecano, where he took a low-budget squad to the Conference League final with an intense, front-foot pressing 4-4-2/4-2-3-1; his opening XI (Luiz Júnior; Mouriño, Foyth, Renato Veiga, Romero; Gueye, Comesaña; Pépé, Moleiro; Ayoze, Mikautadze) kept Marcelino's two-striker shape.

Principles: academy-first (ciudad deportiva, one of spain's most productive), buy-to-develop from ligue 1/south america, stable, family-run governance, no star wages

Character: Town of ~50k with a family-owned, patient, low-drama club culture; expectations calibrated to Europe every year rather than titles; minimal media pressure compared with Madrid/Barcelona.

## Strengths

- Third in La Liga two seasons in a row, finishing above Atlético in 2025/26 with a 5-1 final-day win.
- Squad continuity: the core that finished 3rd is intact and Pérez's opening XI was almost entirely last season's starters.
- Mikautadze (13 league / 15 total goals) is a reliable primary scorer with Ayoze and Gerard Moreno alongside.
- Strong academy pipeline (Cabanes, Rubén Gómez, Diatta promoted) plus a cheap experienced No. 2 keeper in Gulácsi (23 Bundesliga starts in 2025/26).

## Weaknesses

- Only Gulácsi (EUR 1.5m) and a youth signing arrived all summer: a squad that finished 3rd has not been strengthened for a double campaign.
- Champions League 2025/26 was a failure: one point from eight league-phase games (one draw, seven defeats).
- Midfield thinned: Parejo, Partey and Terrats gone, Requena loaned; Gueye-Comesaña have little senior cover.
- Coaching change from Marcelino's counter-attacking 4-4-2 to Pérez's pressing game requires adaptation mid-season; two draws (2-2, 2-2) to open La Liga.
- Loss of Pedraza (loan) thins left-back cover behind Romero and Cardona; Kambwala reportedly leaving.
