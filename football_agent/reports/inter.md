## Identity

Chivu kept Inter's 3-5-2 framework - wing-backs providing width, a regista (Calhanoglu) plus two box-to-box midfielders, and a two-striker partnership led by Lautaro Martinez - while adding more direct verticality. The result was a dominant Scudetto (87 pts, 89 scored, 35 conceded) but a shock round-of-16 Champions League exit to Bodo/Glimt.

Principles: financially constrained excellence (owner oaktree demands sustainability), free transfers and value buys over big fees, experienced serie a-proven core, youth integration gradual (pio esposito, bonny), sell one asset per summer to balance books

Character: Owned since May 2024 by US fund Oaktree after the Suning default; austerity-minded board (Marotta as president) yet consistently competitive; passionate Curva Nord, Milan derby rivalry and the shared San Siro/new-stadium question dominate the media.

## Strengths

- Dominant domestic champion: 87 pts, 27 wins, 89 goals, 11 clear of Napoli - title won with three games to spare
- Settled 3-5-2 system and a coach (Chivu) whom the board is extending; tactical continuity into the UCL
- Elite creative wing-back pair (Dimarco/Dumfries) and a regista (Calhanoglu) that generate set-piece and cut-back volume
- Striker depth across ages: Lautaro/Thuram (29) with Bonny (22) and Pio Esposito (21) developing

## Weaknesses

- Very old core: Sommer 37, Acerbi 38, Mkhitaryan 37, Darmian 36, de Vrij 34, Dimarco 33, Calhanoglu 32 - highest average age among Pot 1 sides
- European fragility: eliminated by Bodo/Glimt in the round of 16 despite domestic dominance
- Oaktree's budget constraints limit the ability to replace the ageing core in one window; net-spend must stay near neutral
- Summer 2026 window activity could not be verified - risk that few of the age-related gaps were addressed
- Left-sided depth: Dimarco (33) has no young understudy; Carlos Augusto is a converted CB
