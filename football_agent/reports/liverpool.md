## Identity

Identity is in transition: Arne Slot's 4-2-3-1 possession-and-press model won the 2024/25 title but collapsed to 5th (60 pts, 53 conceded, 12 defeats) in 2025/26 despite GBP 440m+ of signings, and Slot was dismissed on 30 May 2026. Andoni Iraola (appointed 4 June 2026, two-year deal) brings the high-intensity, man-marking press and relentlessly vertical attacking that took Bournemouth to 6th; he is expected to keep a 4-2-3-1 with short build-up from the keeper and direct play when pressed. The rebuilt front line (Barcola, Muñoz, Wirtz, Isak, Ekitike, Gakpo) suits a transition-heavy game; Salah, Konaté and Robertson all left.

Principles: data-led recruitment (research department under fsg), buy under-25 with resale value; occasional marquee exception, wage discipline relative to revenue, 'this means more' - community and hillsborough legacy, academy pathway (bradley, nyoni, ngumoha)

Character: US private ownership (FSG) with a data-driven, financially prudent model; intense but supportive Anfield atmosphere; club leaning on a sporting-director structure (Richard Hughes, Michael Edwards as FSG CEO of football) rather than a single manager.

## Strengths

- Attacking talent density: Wirtz (23), Isak (26), Ekitike (24, 17 goals in 2025/26), Barcola (23, EUR 123m) and Muñoz (EUR 40m) - one of Europe's deepest front lines on paper
- Young full-backs (Frimpong 25, Kerkez 22, Bradley 23) suit Iraola's high-tempo transition game
- Financial capacity proven by two consecutive EUR 400m+ windows without FFP/PSR breach reports
- Strong sporting-director structure (Hughes/Edwards) providing continuity; Hughes previously hired Iraola at Bournemouth
- Reached the Champions League quarter-final in 2025/26 despite the domestic collapse

## Weaknesses

- Worst defensive record of a top-5 side: 53 conceded and 12 defeats; finished 25 points behind champions Arsenal
- Coaching reset: Slot sacked 30 May 2026; Iraola's man-marking press is a different system to learn during a UCL group with Atlético and Inter
- Centre-back turnover: Konaté left on a free; Van Dijk 35; Jacquet (20, coming off a shoulder injury) and the loanee Araújo are the replacements
- Left-back depth: Robertson left on a free, leaving Kerkez as the only specialist
- Midfield lacks a dominant defensive No.6 - Gravenberch and Mac Allister are progressive profiles; Jones sold, Endo 33
- Squad-cost pressure: multiple EUR 100m+ attackers concentrate wages in one area of the pitch
