## Identity

Gasperini imported his Atalanta model: a 3-4-2-1 that presses man-for-man across the whole pitch, defends on a very advanced line and attacks with extreme dynamism and verticality, with wide centre-backs and wing-backs (Wesley, Molina, Rensch) licensed to arrive in the box. 2025/26 delivered 3rd place (70 pts, 53-35) — a return to the Champions League — but early exits in the Coppa Italia (Torino) and Europa League (Bologna). The 2026 window added a No. 9 who fits the press (Castro), two centre-backs comfortable 1v1 (Koulierakis, Balerdi), a right wing-back (Molina) and Gasperini's old lieutenant De Roon, and Malen's January loan was converted for EUR 25m.

Principles: gasperini system built for man-marking pressers and athletic wide defenders, american private ownership (friedkin) funding a eur 1bn private stadium and a rebuild under uefa settlement limits, argentine core (dybala, soulé, castro, balerdi, molina) around italian veterans (pellegrini, cristante, mancini), sell or loan misfits quickly (dovbyk, vaz, baldanzi, ziolkowski, angeliño), youth from trigoria given senior slots (pisilli, lulli, arena, de marzi)

Character: Owned 95.97% by The Friedkin Group (Dan Friedkin president) since 2020; one of Italy's most passionate and demanding fanbases, with heavy Roman media pressure and a Curva Sud that has repeatedly clashed with ownership over communication. The privately funded 60,605-seat Pietralata stadium (EUR 1.047bn) received Capitoline Assembly feasibility approval in March 2026 with groundbreaking targeted for 2027.

## Strengths

- Return to the Champions League on merit: 3rd in Serie A with 70 points (20-10-8) and only 35 conceded — a top-three defence.
- Clear, proven tactical identity: Gasperini's man-marking press and 3-4-2-1, now reinforced with players who know it (De Roon) or fit it (Castro, Koulierakis, Molina).
- Malen's impact: 14 Serie A goals after arriving in January (15 in 20 apps all comps), bought outright for EUR 25m to 2030.
- Argentine attacking chemistry: Dybala (renewed), Soulé (6 goals, 5 assists), Castro (EUR 35m), plus Molina and Balerdi at the back.
- Retained core: Koné kept despite a EUR ~61m Chelsea approach; Cristante renewed to 2028; Pellegrini and Dybala confirmed.

## Weaknesses

- Cup fragility: knocked out of the Coppa Italia by Torino and the Europa League by Bologna (4-5 agg) despite a strong league season.
- Goals: only 53 scored in 38 league games; the previous No. 9 (Dovbyk) was loaned out and Castro is unproven at UCL level.
- Financial ceiling: UEFA settlement agreement (EUR 3m fine for missed targets) forces loans-with-obligations and ~EUR 2m net salaries.
- Ageing spine: Dybala (contract to 2027), De Roon (35), Pellegrini, Mancini and Cristante are all past 28.
- Goalkeeper depth behind Svilar is thin (Gollini, youth keeper De Marzi); left-back depth after Angeliño's exit relies on Wesley/Hermoso.
- Squad churn: nine or more departures and seven arrivals in one summer under a demanding tactical system with a high injury load.
