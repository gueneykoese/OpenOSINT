## Identity

Pellegrini's Betis play a patient, possession-oriented 4-2-3-1 in which the playmaker (Isco, Lo Celso) links a double pivot to two direct wingers - Antony on the right (12 goals, 9 assists by April 2026) and Abde on the left - with a mobile No. 9 (Cucho, now joined by Parrott). The 2025/26 side drew 15 of 38 league games and conceded 48, finishing 5th on 60 points, but reached the Europa League quarter-finals and secured a first UCL appearance in 21 years via Spain's European performance spot.

Principles: stability: pellegrini since 2020 (copa del rey 2022, conference league final 2025, ucl 2026), balance experienced free/low-fee signings (isco, lo celso, bartra, ceballos free 2026) with young resale assets (bernal eur 9.5-10.5m with eur 50m clause, parrott eur 16m+4m to 2031), sell to fund: altimira to sporting eur 18.2m; loan-to-buy where possible (antony 2025, amrabat returned), nuevo villamarin stadium rebuild - playing at la cartuja meanwhile, cantera pathway (angel ortiz, pablo garcia promoted)

Character: One of Spain's best-supported clubs (60,000-plus season-ticket base) with a famously loyal, emotional fanbase ('Betis manque pierda') and a fierce city rivalry with Sevilla FC. Big-club atmosphere with mid-table historical resources; pressure is high but the boardroom has been unusually stable. Playing temporarily at the Estadio La Cartuja while the Benito Villamarin is rebuilt.

## Strengths

- Elite creativity for a Pot-2 side: Isco, Lo Celso and Fornals behind Antony (12 goals/9 assists by April 2026) and Abde.
- Continuity and calm: Pellegrini's seventh season, contract to 2027, and a stable board; Conference League final (2025) and EL quarter-final (2026) give recent European experience.
- Reinforced spine: Parrott (EUR 16m+4m) adds a second No. 9, Bernal and Ceballos rebuild the pivot after Altimira/Amrabat left.
- Hard to beat in Europe at La Cartuja; big-crowd atmosphere in a 60,000-capacity venue.
- Balanced full-back depth (Bellerin/Ortiz, Fran Garcia/Firpo).

## Weaknesses

- Drew 15 of 38 league games and conceded 48 - a lack of killer instinct and defensive solidity against a UCL schedule (Arsenal, Bayern, Dortmund).
- Ageing core: Isco, Bartra, Bellerin, Lo Celso and Ceballos are all 30-plus - fitness risk over a 50-plus-game season.
- Collapsed from 2-0 up to lose 2-4 to Braga (EL QF) and lost 1-5 at home to Atletico (Copa QF) - concentration under pressure.
- Goalkeeping: Valles plus a loanee back-up after Adrian's retirement.
- Squad-number squeeze (only two free numbers by late August) signals a full, hard-to-trim squad with limited flexibility.
