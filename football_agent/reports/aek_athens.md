## Identity

Under Nikolić AEK won the 2025/26 title on the back of the league's best defence (21 goals conceded in 32 games) while still scoring 72; the team is compact without the ball and attacks quickly through wide players (Zubkov, Kutesa) feeding a target striker (Jović/Varga). Formation was not verifiable from this session's sources.

Principles: experienced-import model (players 26-31 from mid-tier european leagues), domestic title as core objective, european progress used as revenue lever (conference league semi-final 2025/26)

Character: Historic Athens club with a fervent, demanding fan base (Original 21) and a high-pressure Greek media environment; Agia Sophia Stadium (Nea Filadelfeia) opened 2022.

## Strengths

- Best defence in Greece in 2025/26 (21 GA in 32) - a low-block, low-error structure that travels well in Europe (Conference League semi-final).
- Continuity: title-winning coach Nikolić extended to 2029 (June 2026), so the system is stable entering the UCL.
- Midfield depth rebuilt with four experienced arrivals (Majer, Kairinen, Arriaga, Vitális) replacing departed Pineda/Grujić/Jønsson/Ljubičić.
- Home fortress at Agia Sophia; four home UCL games incl. Real Madrid and Roma give revenue and motivation.

## Weaknesses

- Squad is old for a Pot-4 club: core forwards/defenders (Jović, Vida, Moukoudi, Mantalos) are 28-33 - limited resale value and athletic decline risk over a 40+ game season.
- Loss of Pineda's creativity; Majer and Zubkov must adapt immediately to a new league.
- Left-back is thin after Penrice's loan exit (Lykogiannis, 33, arrived on a free).
- Step up from Conference League to UCL Pot-1/2 opposition (City, Real, Dortmund) exposes limited pace in the back line.
