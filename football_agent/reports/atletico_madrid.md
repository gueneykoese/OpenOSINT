## Identity

Simeone's Atlético remain a defence-first team that shifts between a 4-4-2 and a 5-3-2 depending on the opponent, defending compactly and attacking through quick transitions and set pieces. 2025/26 was more open than the Cholo norm (62 scored, 44 conceded in La Liga) and the team was far better in knockout football (UCL semi-final, Copa final) than over 38 league rounds (4th, 69 points, 25 behind Barcelona). The 2026 window added technical quality (Lee Kang-in, Grimaldo, Hjulmand) and a ball-playing captain-type centre-back (Romero) after Griezmann, Molina and Almada left.

Principles: coaching continuity: simeone in charge since december 2011, longest tenure in europe's top five leagues, defensive organisation and intensity as identity ('cholismo'), sustained investment funded by large sales and mls/south american exits (griezmann, almada, ruggeri, molina) plus apollo capital, argentine core (simeone, romero, álvarez, giuliano simeone, musso), mid-season opportunism (lookman, mendoza, vargas signed in february 2026)

Character: Second club of Madrid with a fiercely loyal fanbase and a self-image as the underdog against Real Madrid; 70k Riyadh Air Metropolitano; high-pressure Madrid media environment but unusual stability under a 14-year coaching reign. Ownership is private (Gil Marín / Cerezo holding with Quantum Pacific, plus Apollo Sports Capital investment announced in 2025) with an ambitious Ciudad del Deporte real-estate project around the stadium.

## Strengths

- Knockout pedigree in 2025/26: Champions League semi-finalists (eliminated Tottenham 7-5 and Barcelona 3-2 on aggregate) and Copa del Rey finalists (beat Barcelona 4-3 agg).
- Unmatched coaching continuity: Simeone in charge since December 2011 (800+ matches), giving a stable defensive structure new signings slot into.
- Aggressive 2026 recruitment of proven starters: Romero (EUR 40m), Hjulmand (EUR 40m), Lee Kang-in (EUR 35m), Grimaldo (EUR 20m) plus Lookman (EUR 35m+5m, Feb 2026).
- Deep striker pool: Sørloth (13 La Liga goals), Álvarez, Jonathan David on loan with option; Lookman can also play as a forward.
- Set-piece and delivery upgrade with Grimaldo (left foot) and Lee Kang-in; Giuliano Simeone led the team with 6 La Liga assists.

## Weaknesses

- League consistency: 4th with 69 points, 11 defeats and 44 conceded — 25 points behind Barcelona and behind Villarreal (72).
- Leadership and creativity void after Griezmann's exit (10 seasons, 211 goals) and Koke ageing; Almada moved on after one year.
- Right-back turnover: Molina sold, leaving Pubill and the converted Llorente as the only options.
- Squad integration risk: at least five new first-team starters arriving in one window plus a change of goalkeeper hierarchy (Musso wears No. 1, Oblak No. 13).
- Non-EU slot pressure and fringe-player churn (Vargas frozen out, Carlos Martín loaned) suggest squad-planning strain.
