## Identity

Carrick moved United back to a 4-2-3-1 after Amorim's back-three experiment. The double pivot exchanges short passes with the centre-backs to draw the press and then plays through the gaps; the No.10 (Fernandes) has licence to roam and attempt risky forward passes, with Mbeumo/Cunha/Rashford providing pace either side of a central striker. Out of possession the team defends in a compact block. The summer 2026 window doubled down on the midfield engine (Baleba, Tielemans, Andrey Santos).

Principles: coach continuity after a crisis: carrick promoted from interim (jan 2026) to permanent (may 2026, contract to 2028), ineos/ratcliffe-led football operations with a data-and-recruitment-department model, heavy midfield investment in 2026 (baleba gbp 70m, tielemans, santos) after casemiro's exit and ugarte's injury, academy pathway maintained (mainoo, amass, fletcher twins, lacey, jj gabriel given senior numbers), large-scale squad trimming: 24 exits via sales, loans and expiries in summer 2026

Character: Global mega-club with the largest commercial revenues in England, extreme media scrutiny and impatient expectations after a decade without a league title. 2025/26 was a recovery season (3rd, first UCL return since 2024/25 absence). Old Trafford atmosphere remains politically charged by 'Glazers out' protests; plans for a USD 1-2bn stadium modernisation/new build are led by the INEOS side.

## Strengths

- Stabilised, clearly-defined 4-2-3-1 under Carrick: 11 wins in his first 16 league games turned a crisis season into 3rd place and a UCL return.
- Deepest midfield in years: Baleba (GBP 70m), Tielemans, Andrey Santos join Fernandes and Mainoo, giving ball-winning, progression and box arrivals.
- Multiple double-figure scorers (Sesko 12, Mbeumo 12, Cunha 10+ in all competitions 2025/26) plus Rashford re-integrated as No.9-shirt wearer; 69 league goals.
- Settled goalkeeper: Lammens became undisputed No.1 in his debut season, ending the Onana/Bayindir uncertainty.
- Financial firepower and commercial scale to act quickly in the market (Baleba deal completed 25 Aug 2026).

## Weaknesses

- Defensive availability: De Ligt injured, Martinez only just back from the World Cup, Yoro yet to convince - Maguire (33) remains a guaranteed starter.
- Midfield injury cluster at kick-off: Ugarte (long-term knee) and Baleba (ankle) both out in Aug/Sept 2026.
- 50 league goals conceded in 2025/26 - worst defensive record of the top four - and cup humiliations (Grimsby, Brighton) show vulnerability against lower blocks/upsets.
- Ownership politics (Glazer voting control vs INEOS operations) and stadium debate create constant off-field noise.
- Squad churn: 24 exits and 7+ arrivals in one window; integration burden ahead of a first UCL campaign under Carrick.
