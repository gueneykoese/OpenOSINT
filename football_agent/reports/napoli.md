## Identity

Conte's 2025/26 Napoli (2nd, 76 pts, 7 behind Inter) were an intense, physically demanding side alternating 4-3-3 and 3-5-2 with Rasmus Højlund (12 league goals) as focal point. Allegri, confirmed 3 July 2026 on a contract to 2029, historically prioritises defensive organisation, compactness and pragmatic control over pressing volume; expect fewer high-tempo phases and more emphasis on set pieces and transitions.

Principles: balanced-budget ownership (de laurentiis), buy players in the eur 20-40m band, sell at peak, no over-30 long contracts as policy, coach-driven identity changes

Character: City-club with immense passion and pressure (Stadio Maradona); owner-president Aurelio De Laurentiis is outspoken and highly involved; media environment intense; two Scudetti in 2023 and 2025 raised expectations.

## Strengths

- Second-best team in Italy two seasons running (Scudetto 2025, 2nd with 76 pts, 58-36 in 2025/26).
- Allegri's record of defensive organisation suits a league-phase format where avoiding defeats matters; 4-3-3 with Lobotka-McTominay-Anguissa confirmed in the 2-0 opening win at Genoa.
- Højlund (12 league / 16 total goals in 2025/26) is a young, improving focal point, now owned outright to 2030 with Lucca as cover.
- Core retained and renewed: Meret and Rrahmani to 2027; Lang, Lucca, Rafa Marín all registered on the UEFA list despite earlier exit rumours.
- Financially disciplined ownership; ~EUR 42m of sales covered a large share of the buy-outs.

## Weaknesses

- Coaching change from Conte to Allegri means a new identity is being built during the league phase; fourteen departures in one summer.
- Allegri's teams typically generate lower chance volume; goal supply beyond Højlund (Lucca, Alisson Santos, Neres) is unproven at this level.
- Failed to defend the 2025 title (7 pts behind Inter), exited the Coppa Italia at the quarter-final and went out of the 2025/26 Champions League in the league phase.
- Buongiorno injured and unregistered; centre-back depth relies on Beukema, Rafa Marín and the new loanee Badiashile.
- Left-back: Gutiérrez sold for EUR 30m; Spinazzola and Olivera are the only options.
