## Identity

Bosz's PSV are a possession-dominant, high-pressing 4-3-3 side that controls games through short passing and positional play, with fluid movement in the final third; the 2025/26 champions scored 101 league goals (27-3-4, 84 points, 19 ahead of Feyenoord). The flip side was 45 goals conceded and a 28th-place UCL league-phase exit, so Bosz has publicly made defensive solidity the priority for 2026/27 while keeping the attacking identity; a back-three was tested in pre-season.

Principles: develop-and-sell to the elite: saibari to bayern for ~eur 50m (club record) follows tillman/lang/bakayoko sales, reinvest selectively in eredivisie-proven dutch talent (mijnans eur 13m from az) and cheap experienced back-ups (kovar, drommel), promote from jong psv (merien, van den berg) and keep a young core (babadi, abed, bajraktarevic, van bommel), coach continuity: bosz contracted to mid-2028; gm marcel brands to 2029, three titles in four seasons; ucl participation is a structural revenue pillar

Character: Foundation-owned, corporate-rooted club (founded by Philips) with a stable, low-drama boardroom culture and a loyal, family-oriented fanbase at the Philips Stadion; pressure is about titles and UCL knockout progress rather than survival. Dutch media scrutiny is intense but civil; the club's 2025/26 UCL failure (28th) was the main criticism of a title season.

## Strengths

- Most dominant domestic side in the pool: 84 points and 101 goals in 2025/26, title won with six rounds to spare.
- Proven scorers retained: Pepi (19 goals, 16 in the league) plus Man, Perisic (kept despite exit talk) and Til.
- Elite creator in Veerman (14 Eredivisie assists) with Schouten anchoring midfield.
- Coach and boardroom stability (Bosz to 2028, Brands to 2029) and a clear positional-play identity.
- Record Saibari sale (~EUR 50m) gives financial headroom without weakening the first XI's defence.

## Weaknesses

- Defensive fragility: 45 league goals conceded by a champion and 28th in the UCL league phase; Bosz himself flagged it.
- Saibari's 19 goals from midfield not directly replaced - Mijnans is the only creative arrival.
- Left-back is thin after Salah-Eddine's return to Roma: Mauro Junior is the only natural option (no. 2 shirt left vacant).
- Ageing key attackers (Perisic 37, Plea) against a very young bench (Babadi, Abed, Bajraktarevic).
- Goalkeeping hierarchy unsettled after a summer of changes (Olij vs new signing Kovar).
