# Abdullah Öcalan — Own Statements and Documented Actions, 1949–1989

**Compiled:** 2026-09-06
**Method / limits:** Open web search only (Turkish, Kurdish, English queries). The research environment blocked direct page retrieval (egress proxy denied every fetch, incl. Wikipedia/Wikisource/Rudaw/ANF/Hürriyet/Milliyet/T24), so every entry below rests on search-engine snippets and abstracts of the cited pages, not on full-page reads. Consequences:
- Quotes marked **[verbatim-snippet]** appeared verbatim in a search snippet of the cited source; they are short and should be re-checked against the full page before publication.
- Quotes marked **[paraphrase]** are my rendering of what the snippet reported; **do not treat them as Öcalan's exact words**.
- Where I could not confirm a quote at all I say so ("not verified").
- Reliability scale: **P** = primary (Öcalan's own text/testimony, court record, party organ); **S** = secondary (journalism, academic, biography); **H** = hearsay/allegation without documentary support.
- **⚑ Suspicion flag** = statement is contradictory, self-serving, or is cited by people alleging MİT ties.

Kurdish-language sources: searches on Rudaw Kurmancî, Xwebûn, Basnews with "Ocalan / ajan / MÎT" produced almost nothing period-specific; the only substantive Kurmanji hit was a 2025 essay on "muxalefeta kontrolkirî" (controlled opposition) at rupelanu.com (see §B). Kurdish-movement Turkish-language outlets (ANF, Yeni Özgür Politika, Mezopotamya Ajansı, Hawar News, Yeni Yaşam) are used extensively and are pro-Öcalan by design.

## A. Chronological entries

### 1. 4 April 1949 (disputed: 1946/1948) — Birth, Ömerli (Amara), Halfeti, Urfa
- **Context:** Born to Ömer Öcalan (1907–1975, farmer) and Üveyş Öcalan (1922–1993). Movement outlets treat 4 April 1949 as canonical; Britannica/SAGE list 1948, and some Turkish sources say 1946. Court documents use 1948.
- **Öcalan's own account:** He has repeatedly described his mother as "isyan tufanı" (a "storm of rebellion") and credited her with his combative character; the movement press reproduces these passages from his prison writings. Verbatim text not retrievable here — **not verified**.
- **Sources:** https://www.urfanatik.com/ocalanin-ailesi-kimdir-oglu-kimdir-babasi-ne-is-yapar-hangi-okul-mezunu ; https://rojnews.news/tr/bir-isyanin-portresi-uveys-ocalan/ ; https://yeniyasamgazetesi9.com/abdullah-ocalanin-isyan-tufani-uveys-ocalan/ ; https://www.britannica.com/biography/Abdullah-Ocalan
- **Reliability:** S (birth date) / P (self-description, via movement press).
- **Flag:** none, though the birth-year inconsistency itself is sometimes cited by critics as "manufactured biography" (weak).

### 2. c. 1966–1969 — Ankara Tapu-Kadastro Meslek Lisesi; religious and nationalist phase
- **Context:** Attended the cadastral vocational high school in central Ankara 1966–69. By his own account he was a practising Muslim organizing prayer at school, attended Maltepe Mosque, went to **Ülkü Ocakları** and **Komünizmle Mücadele Derneği** events, and was most impressed by a **Necip Fazıl Kısakürek** conference.
- **Own words:** Hürriyet headline (1999-era) "Gençliğimde ülkücüydüm" ("In my youth I was an ülkücü") **[headline verbatim; body not retrieved]**. Movement/academic summaries report he said his "first application was to Ülkü Ocakları, but they did not take me" — **[verbatim-snippet, from LemanHaber headline: "Benim ilk başvurum Ülkü Ocakları'nadır. Ama almadılar"]**. Barış Pehlivan (Cumhuriyet, 2025) argued these admissions were long public in Öcalan's own books, not a "secret".
- **Sources:** https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/gundem/gencligimde-ulkucuydum-35641 ; https://www.lemanhaber.com/yasam/kultur-sanat-benim-ilk-basvurum-ulku-ocaklarinadir-ama-almadilar-h9063/ ; https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/yazarlar/baris-pehlivan/ocalan-in-anlattigi-sir-degildi-2473813 ; https://tuicakademi.org/op-ed-abdullah-ocalani-okumak-ama-hangisini/ ; https://www.rudaw.net/turkish/middleeast/turkey/1304202515 (photo from ~1970 published 2025)
- **Reliability:** P (self-admission, repeated in defence texts and interviews).
- **⚑ Flag:** Self-admission is used two ways: by Öcalan to show ideological journey; by MİT-tie theorists (Özgürel, Dilipak, Pekmezci) as evidence he was "inside" nationalist/state circles from the start.

### 3. 1966–67 — Alleged employment at "Türkiye Fikir Ajansı" (Avni Özgürel claim)
- **Context:** Journalist Avni Özgürel (interview 2003; repeated later) says he saw the teenage Öcalan working as an office boy ("ofis boy"/"getir-götür") at Türkiye Fikir Ajansı, a body owned by ex-special-warfare officer Refik Korkud (Yiğitbaş), which Özgürel says MİT used to monitor organisations. Korkud appears in post-1960 "örtülü ödenek" (secret fund) documents.
- **Öcalan's response:** none found. **Not verified** as fact; Öcalan's own account of this period mentions religious/nationalist activity, not this job.
- **Sources:** https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/gundem/ocalan-mitte-ofis-boydu-178504 ; https://www.aydinlik.com.tr/koseyazisi/turkiye-fikir-ajansi-ve-refik-korkud-32043 ; https://www.habername.com/haber-ozgurel-ocalan-buyuk-dogu-dergisine-yakindi-81675.htm ; https://t24.com.tr/haber/ajan-olsam-soylerdim-mit-tirlari-haberi-ben-dahil-herkese-servis-edildi-yazmadim,304516
- **Reliability:** H (single eyewitness, decades later, no document).
- **⚑ Flag:** Core plank of the "MİT from adolescence" narrative.

### 4. 1969 — Turn to the left
- **Context:** Öcalan dates his theoretical shift to reading Leo Huberman's *Sosyalizmin Alfabesi* and his practical shift to the 1969 funeral of Yargıtay president İmran Öktem (attacked by Islamists). Worked ~2 years as a cadastral clerk in Diyarbakır (1969–70), then enrolled at Istanbul Law (1970), where he joined the **DDKO** (Devrimci Doğu Kültür Ocakları) Istanbul branch.
- **Own words:** paraphrased in Sencer/Elips/tuicakademi from his defence texts; **[paraphrase]**.
- **Sources:** https://sencerdergi.com/abdullah-ocalan-teroristbasinin-hayati/ ; https://www.elipshaber.com/abdullah-ocalan-kimdir ; https://www.haberler.com/abdullah-ocalan/biyografisi/
- **Reliability:** P/S.
- **Flag:** none.

### 5. 1 Nov 1971 – 31 Oct 1975 — Transfers to Ankara SBF on a Ministry of Finance scholarship
- **Context:** Court records state he studied at Ankara Üniversitesi SBF on a Maliye ve Gümrük Bakanlığı scholarship for the whole period 01.11.1971–31.10.1975 — i.e. **the state scholarship continued after his 1972 imprisonment**.
- **Sources:** https://tr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C3%96calan_davas%C4%B1_gerek%C3%A7eli_karar%C4%B1 ; https://www.nesra.org/abdullah-ocalan-ve-pkk-hakkinda-kamuoyundaki-supheler/
- **Reliability:** P (court record).
- **⚑ Flag:** Uğur Mumcu and later Nesra/Aydınlık cite the uninterrupted scholarship as circumstantial evidence of state protection. Counter: scholarship revocation required an administrative act that often did not happen for short remands.

### 6. 31 March – 7 April 1972 — Şafak leaflet, Mahir Çayan protest, arrest
- **Context:** After Mahir Çayan and comrades were killed at Kızıldere (30 March 1972), Öcalan distributed the *Şafak* bulletin (Perinçek's TİİKP line) at SBF on 31 March during a boycott/protest; detained 7 April, formally arrested 27 April 1972, held ~7 months in **Mamak Askeri Cezaevi**, released late 1972 (exact release date not found).
- **Own words:** In the 1999 trial and defence texts he presents Kızıldere as his "ant" (oath) moment. **[paraphrase]**. Yeni Yaşam: "Mahir Çayan'ın mirası ve Önder Öcalan".
- **Sources:** https://tr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C3%96calan_davas%C4%B1_gerek%C3%A7eli_karar%C4%B1 ; https://yeniyasamgazetesi9.com/mahir-cayanin-mirasi-ve-onder-ocalan/ ; https://www.haberler.com/abdullah-ocalan/biyografisi/ ; https://serbestiyet.com/yazarlar/48yil-mamak-cezaevinde-bir-karar-ani-2539/
- **Reliability:** P (court record) / S.
- **⚑ Flag:** Dilipak (2019) claims he was recruited by MİT "since 1972", i.e. during/after Mamak; no document offered. Mumcu noted that after release "Abdurrahman Ayhan, Pilot Necati Kaya and Fehmi Yılmaz were placed around Öcalan as support elements" (Mumcu's phrasing as relayed by Nesra) — an allegation, not a finding.

### 7. Newroz (March) 1973 — Çubuk Barajı meeting
- **Context:** Öcalan convened ~6 people at the Çubuk dam near Ankara (Ali Haydar Kaytan, Musa Erdoğan, Mustafa Aksakal, Halil Aslan, İsmail Bingöl named by movement press). Haki Karer and Kemal Pir were absent though living with Öcalan (Emek/Bahçelievler). Thesis "Kürdistan sömürgedir" (Kurdistan is a colony) first put to a group.
- **Own words:** Hawar News headline attributes to him: "1973 Newroz'uyla başlayan yolculuk onur savaşıydı" **[headline only; original wording not verified]**.
- **Sources:** https://hawarnews.com/tr/167926950065886 ; https://anf-news.com/guncel/Oecalan-in-gizli-ruhu-haki-karer-108013 ; https://mezopotamyaajansi43.com/tum-haberler/content/view/275860
- **Reliability:** P (movement chronology) — but all participant lists come from the PKK's own histories.
- **Flag:** none.

### 8. Nov 1973 – Dec 1974 — ADYÖD (Ankara Demokratik Yüksek Öğrenim Derneği)
- **Context:** ADYÖD founded Nov 1973; Öcalan elected to its board/presidency in 1974; the association was raided and closed Dec 1974. Öcalan describes ADYÖD as the "experiential" school of his organising (1974–75) and says he then withdrew into his own group work among Ankara students through 1975.
- **Own words:** **[paraphrase]** via Mezopotamya Ajansı / Wikipedia summaries.
- **Sources:** https://mezopotamyaajansi43.com/tum-haberler/content/view/275860 ; https://military-history.fandom.com/wiki/Abdullah_%C3%96calan ; https://tr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C3%96calan_davas%C4%B1_hakk%C4%B1ndaki_savc%C4%B1l%C4%B1k_iddianamesi
- **Reliability:** P/S.
- **Flag:** none.

### 9. 1975 — First programmatic text with Mazlum Doğan and M. Hayri Durmuş
- **Context:** Wikipedia (citing Marcus/others) says Öcalan, Doğan and Durmuş produced a booklet on the aims of a Kurdish revolution in 1975; the fuller "Kürdistan Devriminin Yolu" came 1977–78 (see #14).
- **Sources:** https://military-history.fandom.com/wiki/Abdullah_%C3%96calan (mirrors en.wikipedia) ; https://sk.sagepub.com/ency/edvol/embed/terrorism/chpt/ocalan-abdullah-1948
- **Reliability:** S.
- **Flag:** none.

### 10. 1975–1977 — "Kürdistan Devrimcileri" / "Apocular" / "Ulusalcılar" / "UKO-cular"
- **Context:** The group worked under these labels in Antep, Urfa, Diyarbakır, Mardin, Batman, Elazığ, Bingöl, Tunceli, Kars, Ağrı. First minuted "official" meeting: **1 Jan 1977, Ankara Dikimevi**. In 1976 the group agreed Öcalan would lead, Haki Karer as deputy; Karer moved to Gaziantep.
- **Sources:** https://mezopotamyaajansi43.com/tum-heberler/content/view/275860 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haki_Karer ; https://tr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C3%96calan_davas%C4%B1_hakk%C4%B1ndaki_savc%C4%B1l%C4%B1k_iddianamesi
- **Reliability:** P (indictment + movement chronology agree on dates).
- **Flag:** none.

### 11. c. 1975–1977 — "Pilot" Necati Kaya joins the circle
- **Context:** Necati Kaya, from Ağrı (Tutak), reportedly a former THY pilot, introduced to Öcalan by Abdurrahman Ayhan. Journalistic accounts say he moved with Kesire Yıldırım, attended group meetings, and was introduced by Kesire as "akrabamız, aile dostumuz" ("our relative, family friend"). He reportedly financed the group; an "Ergenekon" case document dated 3 June 2000 (unverified provenance) alleges 10 million TL passed to Öcalan via Pilot at founding. Died 11 Sept 1982 when his crop-dusting plane hit power lines near Manavgat (grave later located in Ankara).
- **Öcalan's own words (later):**
  - 1999 İmralı interrogation/trial: **"Pilot, Özel Harp Dairesi'ne bağlı bir kontrgerilla"** ("Pilot was a counter-guerrilla attached to the Special Warfare Department") **[verbatim-snippet, multiple outlets]**; that they were inseparable "1977 yılının sonuna kadar"; that this made them "devletin adamı... MİT'in adamı" (state's man / MİT's man — Öcalan apparently framing how it *looked*, and citing Mumcu: "Mumcu da MİT'in adamı dedi") **[verbatim-snippet, context uncertain — the phrase "MİT'in adamı olmamız" as relayed by Yeni Şafak may be a garbled transcription; treat with caution]**; and **"Biz en büyük toplantımızı Ocak 1978'de Pilot'un evinde yaptık"** ("We held our biggest meeting in Pilot's house in January 1978") **[verbatim-snippet]**.
  - On his death: **"1982 yılında kullandığı zirai ilaçlama uçağının düştüğünü ve öldüğünü duydum"** **[verbatim-snippet, trial]**.
  - Lawyer meetings (İmralı, 2000s): "Pilot Necati'nin bizimle geçirdiği bir yıl çok önemliydi" and that his death and agent status should be investigated **[paraphrase of Milliyet/Olay53 snippet]**.
  - Reported via Yalçın Küçük: **"MİT bizi kullanmak istedi, ama biz onları daha çok kullandık"** ("MİT wanted to use us, but we used them more") **[snippet attributes to Küçük's account; exact book/page not verified]**.
- **Sources:** https://www.milliyet.com.tr/gundem/iste-ocalanin-can-simidi-pilot-necati-1376058 ; https://www.yenisafak.com/gundem/pilot-necati-apo-baglantisi-601249 ; https://www.ntv.com.tr/turkiye/iste-pilot-necati,l402j4nod0y2yUHCWd4Y7w ; https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/gundem/pkk-nin-gizemli-ismi-pilot-necati-nin-mezari-ankara-da-5712647 ; https://www.umag.org.tr/ugurmumcu/yazilar/31-kim-bu-pilot.html (Mumcu's column "Kim Bu Pilot?..") ; https://m.star.com.tr/yazar/abdullah-ocalan-dosyasiiii-pilot-necati-ve-abdullah-ocalan-yazi-1072326/ ; https://eksisozluk.com/pilot-necati--1271189
- **Reliability:** P for Öcalan's 1999 statements (court transcript quoted by press); H for the financing/"assigned by MİT" claims.
- **⚑ Flag (strong):** Öcalan admits proximity to a man he himself calls a state operative, yet never explains why the group tolerated him until 1978. This is the single most-cited item by MİT-tie proponents. Counter: Öcalan's admission is against interest and consistent with "state penetration" rather than "state creation"; Pilot's death in 1982 removed the only witness.

### 12. 18 May 1977 — Killing of Haki Karer, Gaziantep
- **Context:** Karer (Turkish, from Ordu) shot in a coffeehouse in Antep; PKK blames Alaattin Kaplan of the rival **Sterka Sor** ("Beş Parçacılar"); Kaplan later killed in İskenderun. Movement narrative: PKK founded "as an oath to Haki Karer".
- **Own words:** Öcalan is quoted across ANF as saying the party was "Haki Karer'e ant" / that "PKK, Haki Karer'in yaşamsal kılınmasıdır" **[ANF headline; attribution to Öcalan vs. PKK collective text unclear]**. A PKK memorial text on Karer is analysed in Zan Enstitüsü's *Toplum ve Kuram* (PDF).
- **Sources:** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haki_Karer ; https://anf-news.com/kadin/pkk-haki-karer-in-yasamsal-kilinmasidir-198282 ; https://www.aydinlik.com.tr/koseyazisi/haki-karer-cinayeti-32344 ; https://zanenstitu.org/uploads/dosyalar/Kitaplar/Toplum%20ve%20Kuram/8sayi/ (PDF)
- **Reliability:** S (killing) / P (movement texts).
- **⚑ Flag:** Aydınlık (Yurtçiçek) and others insinuate the killing was used by Öcalan to eliminate a rival and hint at a state role; no evidence beyond timing. Rumour.

### 13. March 1977 onward — "Kürdistan sömürgedir" speaking tour
- **Context:** From March 1977 Öcalan toured Ağrı, Doğubayazıt, Kars-Digor, Dersim, Bingöl, Elazığ, Diyarbakır, Urfa, Antep giving the "colony" thesis. Indictment and movement press agree.
- **Sources:** https://mezopotamyaajansi43.com/tum-haberler/content/view/275860 ; https://tr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C3%96calan_davas%C4%B1_hakk%C4%B1ndaki_savc%C4%B1l%C4%B1k_iddianamesi
- **Reliability:** P.
- **Flag:** none.

### 14. 1977–1978 — "Kürdistan Devriminin Yolu" (Manifesto)
- **Context:** Written by Öcalan (with two comrades per Britannica) in 1977, adopted at Fis 1978 as the party manifesto and distributed publicly after the 30 July 1979 Bucak attack. Core theses: Kurdistan as an inter-state colony; national-democratic revolution; armed struggle; hostility to "primitive nationalism" and to the Turkish left's "social-chauvinism".
- **Own words:** text not retrievable in this session; **not quoted**.
- **Sources:** https://www.britannica.com/biography/Abdullah-Ocalan ; https://firatnews.com/guncel/47-yilda-12-kongre-Inkar-siyaseti-tasfiye-edildi-sira-demokratik-toplum-insasinda-212591 ; https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKK
- **Reliability:** P (document exists; content summarised secondarily).
- **Flag:** none.

### 15. January 1978 — "Biggest meeting" at Pilot Necati's house (Öcalan's own claim)

### 16. 1978 — Marriage to Kesire Yıldırım
- **Context:** Kesire (b. 21 Oct 1951, Karakoçan), daughter of **Ali Yıldırım**, who had worked for MAH/MİT (code name reported as "Çerçi Ali"; said to have left MİT in the early 1970s). Ali Yıldırım opposed the marriage. Sabah (2011) reported a 1970s episode in which Ali Yıldırım introduced Öcalan inside the MİT building as "bizim Kesire'nin nişanlısı" ("our Kesire's fiancé") **[headline claim; sourced to an ex-MİT witness, unverified]**.
- **Öcalan's own words (later):** **"Son derece iyi eğitilmiş biri. Ajan olup olmadığını çözemedim."** ("Extremely well trained. I could not work out whether she was an agent.") **[verbatim-snippet, Habertürk/NTV, date of utterance not given — likely 1990s interviews or İmralı]**. For years he called her "sarı yılan" ("yellow snake"); in *Demokratik Uygarlık Manifestosu* he reportedly wrote that "belki de hain değildi" ("perhaps she was not a traitor") **[paraphrase from Kesire Wikipedia summary; not verified against the book]**. Some outlets claim he told Birand she was a MİT agent — **not verified**.
- **Sources:** https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kesire_Y%C4%B1ld%C4%B1r%C4%B1m ; https://www.haberturk.com/yasam/haber/570606-ocalanin-mitci-kayinpederi ; https://www.ntv.com.tr/turkiye/ocalanin-mitci-kayinpederi,S3BpWXl1MEiKdFWegZcraA ; https://www.sabah.com.tr/gundem/2011/10/18/kayinpederi-mit-binasinda-ocalani-boyle-tanitmis ; https://www.kibrispostasi.com/c36-TURKIYE/n41815-aponun-mitci-kayinpederinin-kod-adi-da-ortaya-cikti ; https://www.gazetevatan.com/gundem/ocalanin-karisi-mit-miydi-147930
- **Reliability:** S for Ali Yıldırım's MİT past (multiple outlets, apparently from Mumcu's research + 2011 leaks); P for Öcalan's quote.
- **⚑ Flag (strong):** Öcalan's own uncertainty ("çözemedim") is contradictory with his simultaneous branding of her as traitor; used by both sides.

### 17. 18–19 May 1978 — Killing of Halil Çavgun, Hilvan; feud with Süleymanlar
- **Context:** Çavgun (ex-Aydınlık sympathiser, Apocu since mid-1970s) killed in clashes with the Süleymanlar tribe in Hilvan. Kemal Pir, Mehmet Karasungur and Cemil Bayık organised "revenge": tribal leader Mehmet Baysal and others killed. Hilvan municipality later won by an Apocu-backed independent (1979).
- **Own words:** none found for the period; later PKK texts frame Hilvan as the "first resistance".
- **Sources:** https://www.ozgurpolitika.com/haberi-halil-cavgunun-intikami-nasil-alindi-149930 ; https://anf-news.com/kadin/apocular-halil-Cavgun-un-intikamini-nasil-aldi-156574 ; https://www.aydinlik.com.tr/koseyazisi/pkknin-ilk-eylemleri-hilvanda-basliyor-33009 ; https://serbestiyet.com/haberler/ozel-dem-parti-icin-hilvani-kazanmanin-anlami-ne-45-yillik-mesele-169362/
- **Reliability:** S.
- **⚑ Flag:** Burkay's camp cites the Hilvan/Siverek feuds as "state-provoked Kurd-on-Kurd violence" (see §B).

### 19. 26–27 Nov 1978 — Founding congress, Fis (Ziyaret) village, Lice
- **Context:** 22 present (24 planned; Karasungur and Kemal Pir — the latter in prison — absent). Adopted programme, statute, manifesto; name "Partiya Karkerên Kurdistan". Named participants: Öcalan, Cemil Bayık, M. Hayri Durmuş, Mazlum Doğan, Duran Kalkan, Ali Haydar Kaytan, Kesire Yıldırım, Şahin Dönmez (later "traitor"), Çetin Güngör (Semir), etc.
- **Own words:** Yeni Özgür Politika ran participants' recollections; Öcalan's own congress speech text not retrieved.
- **Sources:** https://yeniozgurpolitika.net/katylanlaryn-anlatymyyla-27-kasym-1978-fis-koyu/ ; https://darkamazi.site/archives/1027728 ("Where are the 22 founders?") ; https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKK ; https://hawarnews.com/tr/1978den-bugune-pkk-kongreleri
- **Reliability:** P (movement) / S.
- **⚑ Flag:** Darka Mazî's tally that most founders were later killed, expelled or branded agents is used by critics (Sakık, Burkay) as evidence of a leader who "consumed" his founders.

### 20. 1979 — Kemal Burkay/TKSP pamphlet: PKK = state front
- **Context:** After PKK members killed Mustafa Çamlıbel in Doğubayazıt (1979), Burkay's Özgürlük Yolu/TKSP distributed ~20,000 copies of a pamphlet calling the PKK "devletin Kürt ulusal hareketine karşı kurdurduğu, ajan-provokatörlerce yönetilen bir paravan örgüt" ("a front organisation set up by the state against the Kurdish national movement, run by agent-provocateurs") **[paraphrase of widely repeated summary; pamphlet title not found]**.
- **Öcalan's response then:** PKK texts of the period attacked Burkay's group as "reformist/ilkel milliyetçi"; no direct rebuttal text retrieved.
- **Sources:** https://www.nesra.org/apocular-pkk-ozgurluk-yolu-tksp-catismasi/ ; https://www.milliyet.com.tr/siyaset/kemal-burkayin-bu-aciklmalari-pkkyi-cileden-cikaracak-1508468 ; https://www.tustav.org/sureli-yayinlar-arsivi/ozgurluk-yolu/
- **Reliability:** S (contemporary pamphlet existed; content known via later summaries).
- **⚑ Flag:** the earliest organised MİT-creation allegation; see §B.

### 21. 2–4 July 1979 — Crossing to Syria via Suruç → Kobanî
- **Context:** Movement sources give 2 July; academic (McKeever) gives 4 July 1979, arranged through Suruç cadre Ethem Akcan's relatives in Kobanî and a smuggler. First contacts: Abdi Nasan (Syrian Kurdish Left Party), then the DFLP and other Palestinian groups in Lebanon. Trigger per several accounts: capture of a leading cadre (Şahin Dönmez, May 1979) and Öcalan's belief MİT had infiltrators and was closing in.
- **Own words:** Öcalan later called the 2 July 1979 departure **"zamanında ve yerinde bir adım"** ("a timely and appropriate step") **[verbatim-snippet, source: Medyascope/Wikipedia summary; original text not verified]**. He has also said the move was made anticipating a coup — I could not find a period-contemporary statement to that effect; the "foresaw 12 Eylül" framing appears only retrospectively. **Not verified.**
- **Sources:** https://akmckeever.substack.com/p/the-entrance-of-the-kurdistan-workers ; https://medyascope.tv/2024/12/11/ocalanin-suriye-yillari-suriye-kurtlerini-nasil-etkiledi/ ; https://www.ntv.com.tr/turkiye/ocalanin-suriye-gunleri,77NujxU7SUqYDTUvqCNGug ; https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKK
- **Reliability:** S (well documented) / P (self-assessment).
- **⚑ Flag (strong):** Leaving 14 months before the coup, alone among leftist leaders, is the second most-cited "circumstantial" item by MİT-tie proponents ("he was tipped off"). Counter: Şahin Dönmez's arrest and confessions (May 1979) gave an ordinary reason to flee; the general 1979–80 escalation made a coup foreseeable to many.

### 22. 30 July 1979 — Attack on AP deputy Mehmet Celal Bucak, Kırbaşı (Hilvan)
- **Context:** Ordered (per Turkish sources) by Öcalan from Syria "to announce the party". Bucak escaped; his 8-year-old son Fatih wounded; a child killed; several dead in the wider clash (numbers vary). The manifesto was distributed with the claim of responsibility.
- **Own words:** none contemporaneous found.
- **Sources:** https://www.urfanatik.com/haber/3574347/terorun-ilk-magduru-fatih-bucak ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Kurdistan_Workers'_Party ; https://www.cnnturk.com/turkiye/karayilandan-bucak-asiretine-mesaj-99191
- **Reliability:** S.
- **⚑ Flag:** Burkay reads Siverek as "state-encouraged tribal war"; Bucak was an AP deputy, which cuts against the "state asset" reading.

### 23. 12 Sept 1980 — Coup; Öcalan in Syria/Lebanon
- **Context:** Before the coup only small numbers had gone to Syria for training; after it, ~300 cadres settled in the Bekaa (1980–82) under Palestinian training and covert Syrian tolerance. Inside Turkey the mass arrests fed Diyarbakır Prison.
- **Own words on the coup:** Contemporary PKK position (1st Conference, July 1981, published Serxwebûn Jan 1982) treated the junta as "faşist cunta" and blamed the party's own "tasfiyeci" errors for losses **[paraphrase; source: Yeni Yaşam summary of Serxwebûn 2nd series]**. Later (2010s) Öcalan said "AKP'nin kökeni 12 Eylül'e dayanıyor" **[headline, Mezopotamya Ajansı]** — outside period. No period-contemporary Öcalan quote about the coup could be verified.
- **Sources:** https://yeniyasamgazetesi9.com/pkk-serxwebun-ve-mazlum-dogan/ ; https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKK ; http://mezopotamyaajansi35.com/tum-haberler/content/view/146244
- **Reliability:** P (party organ, via summary).
- **Flag:** none.

### 25. 21 March 1982 / 18 May 1982 — Mazlum Doğan's death; "Dörtler" self-immolation, Diyarbakır Prison

### 28. 2 Nov 1985 — Killing of Çetin Güngör (Semir), Stockholm
- **Context:** Founding CC member, leader of the most systematic internal opposition after 1980, shot at a Kurdish meeting in Stockholm in front of hundreds; the shooter was caught on the spot and told the court he was glad to have carried out Öcalan's order; PKK claimed responsibility as "Avrupa İnfaz Timleri".
- **Öcalan's own words:** In *PKK'ye Dayatılan Tasfiyecilik ve Tasfiyeciliğin Tasfiyesi* (1993) he devotes a chapter (from ~p. 35) to Semir as a "tasfiyeci" and traitor who tried to hollow out the party and push it to capitulation **[paraphrase; de.wikipedia summary]**. Yeni Şafak: Öcalan accused those who criticised the executions of being agents/liquidators **[headline-level]**. Kesire, in her 1988 letter, accused him of eliminating comrades "en acımasız ve anlaşılmaz biçimlerde, sahte suçlamalarla... haklı bir gerekçeniz ve kanıtınız yokken, kimini tutuklayıp kimini fiziksel olarak tasfiye ettiniz" **[verbatim-snippet as relayed by haber3/X; letter authenticity asserted by PKK dissidents, not independently verified]**.
- **Sources:** https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%87etin_G%C3%BCng%C3%B6r_(Parteifunktion%C3%A4r) ; https://www.nesra.org/pkk-infazlar-9-semir-cetin-gungor/ ; https://www.yenisafak.com/gundem/ocalan-infazlari-elestirenleri-ne-ile-suclamis-382982 ; https://www.haberturk.com/gundem/haber/811122-kanli-bilanco (2013 parliamentary list of internal executions) ; https://www.haber3.com/guncel/kesirenin-ocalana-son-mektubu-haberi-157674
- **Reliability:** S (killing, Swedish court) / P (Öcalan's 1993 text, via summary).
- **⚑ Flag:** Öcalan's justification is self-serving; Burkay and Sakık cite the Semir killing as proof the PKK's real targets were Kurds.

**Overall assessment for 1949–1989:** The documented record supports (a) Öcalan's own admitted nationalist/Islamist youth, (b) sustained early contact with at least one man he himself calls a special-warfare operative (Pilot Necati), (c) marriage into the family of a former MAH/MİT employee, and (d) an exit to Syria before the coup. None of this shows MİT *created or directed* the PKK; all of it is compatible with the ordinary pattern of a 1970s Turkish radical group heavily penetrated by the state. The strongest "creation" claims (Burkay 1979/2012, Pekmezci, Dilipak) rest on inference and interested testimony, and Öcalan's own 1999 admissions — made under threat of execution — cut in both directions: he conceded the contacts while denying subordination. Treat every "MİT kurdurdu" statement as allegation, not finding.
