# Part 4 — Abdullah Öcalan's own statements, Jan 2024 – Sep 2026, and the MİT dimension

Compiled 6 September 2026 (OSINT desk research).

## 0. Method, sourcing and limits — read first

- **Collection method.** Web search (Turkish/English queries; Kurdish-language queries were queued but the session's search quota was exhausted before they ran) followed by attempted full-page retrieval. **Every full-page fetch (bianet, medyascope, DEM Parti, Rudaw, ANF, TBMM, T24, Euronews TR, Sözcü, Cumhuriyet, Wikipedia, etc.) was refused by the network egress proxy (HTTP 403 on CONNECT).** Consequently:
  - Quotes marked **[SNIP]** are verbatim as they appeared in the search-index excerpt of the cited page (high confidence in wording, medium confidence in completeness).
  - Quotes marked **[MEM]** are reproduced from the analyst's prior knowledge of widely republished texts and **must be checked against the cited URL before any external use**.
  - Items marked **[UNVERIFIED]** rest on a single or partisan source and could not be cross-checked.
- **Verbatim full texts.** The complete Turkish text of the 27 Feb 2025 call is on bianet (304959) and demparti.org.tr (20769); the 27 Feb 2026 text on bianet (317145), Medyascope and Diken; the 24 Nov 2025 TBMM-commission transcript (16 pp.) on tbmm.gov.tr and bianet (315975). The Kurdish (Kurmancî) version, "Banga Aştî û Civaka Demokratîk", read by Ahmet Türk on 27 Feb 2025, is on ANF Kurdî / Xwebûn; it could not be retrieved here and is therefore **not reproduced** rather than guessed.
- **Reliability scale used below:** A = official/primary (TBMM, party press statement, lawyer statement); B = mainstream outlet quoting A directly; C = partisan or single-source; D = anonymous leak / social-media claim.
- **Suspicion flag** = the entry (i) contradicts a documented earlier Öcalan position (1978 *Kürdistan Devriminin Yolu* manifesto: independent, united, socialist Kurdistan; 1990s independence rhetoric; 1993/1999 turn; 2013 Newroz letter "silahlar sussun, fikirler ve siyaset konuşsun"), or (ii) is cited by critics as evidence of state/MİT scripting. A flag is an analytic marker, not a finding.

## 1. Chronological entries

### 2024

**E01 — Jan–Sep 2024 — Prolonged total isolation; no statements.**
Context: Öcalan's last lawyer visit was Aug 2019; last family visit 3 Mar 2020; last phone call 25 Mar 2021. Through Sep 2024 Asrın Hukuk Bürosu and family repeatedly reported "mutlak tecrit" and applied to the CPT/ECtHR; no Öcalan statement of any kind reached the public in this window.
Source: Asrın Hukuk Bürosu bulletins (asrinhukuk.org, 2024) [MEM]; summary at bianet "Meclis'teki tokalaşmadan 'tarihi çağrı'ya" https://bianet.org/haber/meclis-teki-tokalasmadan-tarihi-cagri-ya-surecte-neler-yasandi-304956.
Reliability: A/B. Suspicion flag: none (absence of statements).

**E02 — 1 Oct 2024 — Bahçeli shakes hands with DEM MPs at TBMM opening.**
Context: MHP leader Devlet Bahçeli walks to the DEM Party benches and shakes hands; widely read as the opening signal. Not an Öcalan statement; included as the trigger.
Source: bianet 304956 (above). Reliability: B. Flag: n/a.

**E03 — 22 Oct 2024 — Bahçeli's call for Öcalan to speak in TBMM.**
Quote [SNIP]: "Teröristbaşı işin içinde olmazsa bir şey çıkmaz diyenlere de sesleniyorum; şayet teröristbaşının tecridi kaldırılırsa gelsin TBMM'de DEM Parti grup toplantısında konuşsun, terörün tamamen bittiğini, örgütün lağvedildiğini haykırsın. … Bu dirayet ve kararlılığını gösterirse umut hakkının kullanımıyla ilgili yasal düzenlemenin yapılması ve bundan yararlanmasının önü de ardına kadar açılsın."
Gloss: "If the chief terrorist's isolation is lifted, let him come and speak at the DEM group meeting in Parliament and shout that terror is over and the organisation is dissolved … then let the way be opened for a legal arrangement on the 'right to hope' and for him to benefit from it."
Sources: Euronews TR https://tr.euronews.com/2024/10/22/devlet-bahceli-pkk-lideri-ocalani-mecliste-konusma-yapmaya-cagirdi ; Yetkin Report https://yetkinreport.com/2024/10/22/bahceliden-ters-kose-ocalan-ciksin-mecliste-konussun/ ; soL https://haber.sol.org.tr/haber/bahcelinin-ocalan-cagrisina-ilk-tepkiler-22-ekim-bir-milattir-395673
Reliability: A (group speech, televised). Flag: **Evidence-of-coordination flag** — critics (Özdağ, Dervişoğlu, see §2) treat the fact that the *state's* nationalist partner, not Öcalan, opened the sequence as proof that the subsequent Öcalan texts were solicited.

**E04 — 23 Oct 2024 — Family visit by nephew Ömer Öcalan (DEM MP); first message in 43 months.**
Quote [SNIP, VOA/Medyascope]: "İzolasyon devam ediyor. Koşullar oluşursa bu süreci çatışma ve şiddet zemininden hukuki ve siyasi zemine taşıyacak teorik ve pratik güce sahibim."
Gloss: "Isolation continues. If conditions are created, I have the theoretical and practical power to move this process from the ground of conflict and violence to a legal and political ground."
Ömer Öcalan added [SNIP]: "Aile olarak yasal bir hak olan rutin aile görüşlerinin her koşulda devam etmesini istiyoruz."
Sources: VOA Türkçe https://www.voaturkce.com/a/ocalan-mesaj-gonderdi-kosullar-olusursa-sureci-hukuki-ve-siyasi-zemine-tasiyacak-guce-sahibim/7836946.html ; Medyascope https://medyascope.tv/2024/10/24/omer-ocalan-acikladi-abdullah-ocalanla-ilk-gorus-gerceklesti/ ; Agos https://www.agos.com.tr/tr/yazi/31196/ocalan-ile-yillar-sonra-ilk-temas-kosullar-olusursa
Reliability: B (relayed orally by a relative/MP; no written text). Flag: timing — visit granted 24 h after Bahçeli's speech; critics cite this as state choreography; DEM says the visit request had been pending for years.

**E05 — 28 Dec 2024 — First DEM "İmralı Heyeti" visit (Sırrı Süreyya Önder, Pervin Buldan).**
Quote [SNIP, Yetkin Report]: "Sayın Bahçeli'nin ve Sayın Erdoğan'ın güç verdiği yeni paradigmaya, ben de pozitif anlamda gerekli katkıyı sunacak ehil ve kararlılığa sahibim." Also [SNIP]: "Türk-Kürt kardeşliğini yeniden güçlendirmek tarihi bir sorumluluk[tur]."
Gloss: "I too have the competence and determination to make the necessary positive contribution to the new paradigm that Mr Bahçeli and Mr Erdoğan have empowered."
Sources: Yetkin Report https://yetkinreport.com/2024/12/29/ocalanin-bahceli-ve-erdogani-selamladigi-mesajinda-iki-onemli-cumle/ ; Gerçek Gündem https://www.gercekgundem.com/siyaset/ikinci-imrali-gorusmesi-sonrasi-teror-orgutu-pkk-lideri-ocalandan-ilk-mesaj-515027
Reliability: A/B (written note read by DEM). **Suspicion flag:** first known instance of Öcalan naming Bahçeli honorifically and crediting the AKP-MHP bloc with a "paradigm" — a reversal of his 1990s characterisation of MHP as "faşist" and of the 2013 letter, which attributed the process to "the peoples", not to state leaders. Yetkin's reading: the two sentences signal that Öcalan accepts the state's framing.

### 2025

**E06 — 22 Jan 2025 — Second DEM visit (Önder, Buldan; Ahmet Türk absent, ill).**
Content [SNIP, ANKA]: condolences for Bolu hotel-fire victims; "Öcalan'ın sürece ilişkin çalışmaları devam etmektedir"; Öcalan said he was ready to present a "yol haritası" out of the "kısır döngü" in which security spending drains workers, poverty deepens, lawlessness becomes routine and hostility to women and children grows.
Source: ANKA https://ankahaber.net/haber/detay/dem_parti_imrali_heyetinin_ikinci_gorusme_sonrasi_aciklama_ocalanin_surece_iliskin_calismalari_devam_etmektedir_216777
Reliability: A/B. Flag: none.

**E07 — 4 Feb 2025 — DEM: "Öcalan tarihi bir çağrıya hazırlanıyor, top Erdoğan'da."**
Context: DEM statements after further contacts; Öcalan reported to have said (via the delegation) that the call would be made once "koşullar" allowed and that other parties (Kandil, KCK, Europe) were being consulted through the delegation's shuttle visits.
Source: Euronews TR https://tr.euronews.com/2025/02/04/dem-parti-ocalan-tarihi-bir-cagriya-hazirlaniyor-artik-top-erdoganda
Reliability: B. Flag: none.

**E08 — 27 Feb 2025 — "Barış ve Demokratik Toplum Çağrısı" (Call for Peace and a Democratic Society).**
Context: Third visit (Önder, Buldan, Ahmet Türk) on 27 Feb; text read at Istanbul Sheraton press conference the same afternoon, Turkish by Önder, Kurdish by Ahmet Türk, before a projected photograph of Öcalan seated with the delegation (first new photo since 1999).
Key verbatim lines (Turkish) — [MEM]; full text at the URLs below:
- "Tüm grupların silahlarını bırakmasını ve PKK'nin kendisini feshetmesini çağırıyorum." ("I call on all groups to lay down arms and on the PKK to dissolve itself.")
- "Demokratik siyasetin ve hukukun uygulandığı yerde silahların bir anlamı kalmamaktadır." (confirmed as the message's core by Medyascope 2026 anniversary piece [SNIP])
- "Ayrı ulus-devlet, federasyon, idari özerklik ve kültüralist çözümler tarihsel toplum sosyolojisine cevap olamamaktadır." [SNIP — quoted in Rupela Nû critique] ("A separate nation-state, federation, administrative autonomy and culturalist solutions cannot answer historical social sociology.")
- "Sayın Devlet Bahçeli'nin ortaya koyduğu irade, Sayın Cumhurbaşkanı'nın gösterdiği irade ve diğer siyasi partilerin olumlu yaklaşımlarıyla oluşan ortamda… bu çağrıyı yapıyorum." [MEM]
- "PKK … Kürt kimliğinin inkârı … sonucu ortaya çıkmıştır… varlık nedeni ortadan kalkmıştır… Kongrenizi toplayın ve karar alın… Bu çağrının tarihi sorumluluğunu üstleniyorum." [MEM]
Kurdish version title: "Banga Aştî û Civaka Demokratîk" (ANF Kurdî / Xwebûn, 27 Feb 2025) — text not retrieved; not reproduced.
Sources: bianet full text https://bianet.org/haber/abdullah-ocalanin-baris-ve-demokratik-toplum-cagrisi-304959 ; DEM Parti https://www.demparti.org.tr/tr/baris-ve-demokratik-toplum-cagrisi/20769/ ; Diken https://www.diken.com.tr/ocalanin-aciklamasinin-tam-metni/ ; RFE/RL via GlobalSecurity https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/turkey/2025/turkey-250227-rferl01.htm ; WSWS TR https://www.wsws.org/tr/articles/2025/02/28/gqwk-f28.html
Reliability: A (written, signed, read publicly). **Suspicion flags:** (1) Explicit renunciation of nation-state, federation *and* autonomy contradicts the 1978 manifesto (independent Kurdistan) and 1990s rhetoric; even the post-1999 "Democratic Republic" texts had kept "democratic autonomy". (2) Attribution of the "ortam" to Bahçeli/Erdoğan's "irade" — cited by Dervişoğlu ("çağrı yaptırıldı") and PSK writers as proof of scripting. (3) Rebuttal: the "historical sociology" argument is a continuation of Öcalan's own 2004-2015 prison writings (*Demokratik Uygarlık Manifestosu*), so the ideological line is at least a decade old, not new.

**E09 — 28 Feb 2025 — CHP leader Özgür Özel: "Müzakereler bir yıldır sürüyor."**
Context: Özel says talks between the state and Öcalan had been under way for about a year before Bahçeli's speech — i.e., the state (read: MİT) ran the channel. Not an Öcalan statement; recorded as the origin of the "MİT-scripted" reading from the main opposition.
Source: Medyascope https://medyascope.tv/2025/02/28/ozgur-ozel-ocalanin-aciklamasiyla-ilgili-konustu-ozgur-ozel-ocalanin-cagrisi-hakkinda-ne-dedi/
Reliability: B. Flag: evidence-of-coordination (claimant CHP).

**E10 — 1 Mar 2025 — PKK Executive Committee declares ceasefire, demands Öcalan's freedom.**
Quote [SNIP]: "Çağrının içeriğini tümüyle destekliyor, gereklerini kendi cephemizden yerine getirip uygulayacağımızı belirtiyoruz… saldırı olmazsa hiçbir gücümüz silahlı eylem yapmayacaktır." And: the congress must be "bizzat Önder Apo tarafından yönetilmesi" and Öcalan must gain "özgür yaşam ve çalışma koşulları".
Sources: Yetkin Report https://yetkinreport.com/2025/03/01/pkk-ateskes-ilan-etti-ocalanin-yonetmesi-gerekir/ ; Euronews TR https://tr.euronews.com/2025/03/01/pkk-ocalanin-cagrisi-ardindan-ateskes-ilan-ettigini-duyurdu ; Hürriyet https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/gundem/son-dakika-teror-orgutu-pkk-ateskes-ilan-etti-42712753
Reliability: A (organisation statement via ANF). Flag: none for Öcalan; note the PKK conditioned compliance on Öcalan's release — the state never granted it, which matters for later "was he coerced?" arguments.

**E11 — 25 & 27 Apr 2025 — Two written Öcalan documents to the PKK 12th Congress.**
Content [SNIP, Medyascope/Rudaw]: comprehensive reasoning for dissolution; the step's "karşılığı" from the state should be "demokratik siyaset hakkının tanınması ve sağlam bütünlüklü bir hukuki güvence" (recognition of the right to democratic politics and a solid, integral legal guarantee). Second letter title per Medyascope: "Fesih gereklidir." A YouTube-circulated version has Öcalan scolding cadres "Beni anlamıyorsunuz" [C, unverified].
Sources: Medyascope https://medyascope.tv/2025/06/04/ocalan-dan-pkkye-ikinci-mektup-surecin-karsiligi-demokratik-siyaset-hakki-ve-hukuki-guvence/ ; Rudaw TR https://www.rudaw.net/turkish/middleeast/turkey/0406202511 ; Medyascope "PKK Kongresi'nde ne konuşuldu?" https://medyascope.tv/2025/06/04/pkk-kongresinde-ne-konusuldu/
Reliability: B (published by PKK organs 4 Jun 2025). Flag: **suspicion** — documents reached Qandil from a "total isolation" prison; the only plausible carrier is state-sanctioned (DEM delegation with MİT logistics), which critics cite as proof the state co-authored the process. Rebuttal: the delegation openly acknowledged carrying letters; carriage ≠ authorship.

**E12 — 3 May 2025 — Condolence message for Sırrı Süreyya Önder (d. 3 May).**
Quote [SNIP]: Önder was "çok değerli bir insan, halkların gerçek bir evladı"; "Anadolu ve Türk geleneği büyük bir evladını, coğrafyamızdaki tüm topluluklar ve halklar büyük bir yoldaşını kaybetti"; "Gerçek bir barış kimliği ve barış kültürüydü." Öcalan recalled that on 27 Feb Önder "wrote down the last sentence of the call and wanted to read it himself." Later (2025) to the delegation: "Sırrı Süreyya Önder ile son bir defa konuşmamak içimde ukde kaldı" [SNIP, Serbestiyet].
Sources: Medyascope https://medyascope.tv/2025/05/03/ocalan-dan-sirri-sureyya-onder-icin-taziye-mesaji-gercek-bir-baris-kimligi-ve-baris-kulturuydu/ ; Hürriyet https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/gundem/ocalandan-sirri-sureyya-onder-icin-taziye-mesaji-42787816 ; Serbestiyet https://serbestiyet.com/haberler/pervin-buldan-ve-av-ozgur-erol-ocalanla-gorusecek-208326/
Reliability: A/B. Flag: none. Note the unusual phrase "Türk geleneği … büyük bir evladını" — an identity framing Öcalan would not have used pre-1999.

**E13 — 5–7 May 2025 — PKK 12th Congress; 12 May announcement of dissolution and end of armed struggle.**
Context: Congress held in two locations in the Medya Defence Zones; decision text says PKK "örgütsel yapısını feshetmiş, silahlı mücadele yöntemine son vermiştir", and that the process "Önder Apo tarafından yönetilecek".
Sources: Euronews TR https://tr.euronews.com/2025/05/12/pkk-kendini-feshettigini-duyurdu ; SETA https://www.setav.org/teror-orgutu-pkknin-fesih-karari
Reliability: A (organisation). Flag: n/a.

**E14 — 13–18 May 2025 — Öcalan's first reaction after the congress (via DEM/lawyers).**
Quote [SNIP, Sözcü/Medyascope]: "Tarihi 12. kongrede alınan kararları ve önümüzdeki döneme ilişkin mesajları saygıyla selamlıyorum." He commemorated Ali Haydar Kaytan and Rıza Altun (founding cadres, deaths announced at the congress). The DEM İmralı delegation (Buldan, Erol) visit on 18 May 2025 conveyed a further message that "süreç ikinci aşamaya geçmiştir" [MEM — check].
Sources: Sözcü https://www.sozcu.com.tr/ocalan-dan-pkk-nin-fesih-karari-sonrasi-ilk-aciklama-p173576 ; Medyascope https://medyascope.tv/2025/05/13/abdullah-ocalan-ali-haydar-kaytan-ve-riza-altunu-andi/
Reliability: B. Flag: none.

Claimant: journalist İsmail Küçükkaya (Halk TV). Quote [SNIP]: "Kaynaklarımız bizi yanıltmıyor ise, İbrahim Kalın görüşmüş efendim, Öcalan'la. Bu bilgi teyide muhtaç olabilir." **E16 — 9 Jul 2025 — First video/audio message in 26 years (recorded June 2025, aired by ANF).**
Setting [SNIP]: Öcalan seated with six other İmralı prisoners; 7-minute recording.
Quotes [SNIP]:
- "Silahın değil, siyasetin ve toplumsal barışın gücüne inanıyorum. Ve sizi de bu ilkeyi hayata geçirmeye çağırıyorum."

- "Silah bırakma mekanizması sürecini ilerleteceğiz."

- [MEM] "Ulus-devlet hedefinden ve silahlı mücadele stratejisinden tamamen vazgeçilmiştir." (Al Jazeera gloss: "the organisation had completely renounced its nation-state goal and armed-struggle strategy.")
- [MEM] He asked for "Meclis'te bir komisyon" to supervise the process.
Gloss: "I believe in the power of politics and social peace, not of weapons… This is a voluntary transition from the armed-struggle phase to the phase of democratic politics and law; it must be seen not as a loss but as a historic gain."

Reliability: A (audio-visual, authenticated by DEM/ANF). **Suspicion flags:** (1) explicit renunciation of the nation-state goal vs. 1978 manifesto; (2) the recording was made inside a maximum-security prison and released via a PKK outlet — impossible without state (MİT/Justice Ministry) consent, cited by nationalists as proof of "ortak yapım"; rebuttal: the state has an obvious interest in the disarmament message, which explains consent without implying authorship.

### Uncontested facts that feed the allegation (and what they do and do not show)
1. MİT ran the channel: Kalın briefed party leaders (Jan–Feb 2025), MİT attended/observed the Sulaymaniyah ceremony, and every İmralı visit, letter and the June 2025 video required Justice Ministry/MİT clearance. **Shows** the state facilitated the messaging; **does not show** the state wrote it.
2. Öcalan's content shifted to praise state actors (E05, E08, E22, E29). **Shows** a deliberate political choice; consistent with his post-1999 line, so not a 2024 novelty.
3. The state has given Öcalan nothing personal (no release, no umut hakkı, E27/E41) and he has endorsed the law anyway (E36). **Cuts against** a quid-pro-quo "asset" model; **supports** either a genuine ideological conviction or a longer-term bargain not yet visible.
4. Öcalan himself has now publicly disowned quotes (E38) — the first hard evidence that some circulating "Öcalan statements" are fabrications, which should raise the bar for any allegation built on leaked notes.
