# Part 3 — Abdullah Öcalan's own statements from İmralı, 2000–2023

**Method note (read first).** This section was compiled under two hard constraints: (a) the research session's web-search quota was exhausted after ~45 queries, and (b) the egress proxy blocked direct page fetches from every Turkish/Kurdish news domain tried (bianet, T24, Cumhuriyet, Hürriyet, Evrensel, Rudaw, ANF, Medyascope, Euronews TR, Wikipedia, Yeni Şafak, sendika.org). Consequently:

- Quotes marked **[VERBATIM – snippet]** were returned word-for-word inside search-engine result summaries of the cited page. They are reliable as to wording but were not checked against the full page.
- Quotes marked **[PARAPHRASE]** are summaries from search snippets or from well-established public record; wording is *not* to be cited as Öcalan's exact words.
- Items marked **[UNVERIFIED THIS SESSION]** rest on general background knowledge; the URL given is the best-known primary/secondary source and should be re-fetched before publication.
- Nothing has been invented. Where I could not find a quote, I say so.

Reliability scale: **A** = primary text (letter/book/lawyer note reproduced in full by a mainstream outlet); **B** = mainstream secondary report with direct quotes; **C** = partisan outlet or single-source claim; **D** = anonymous/leaked/disputed material.

Suspicion flag: 🚩 = statement or episode cited (by critics, dissidents, or Turkish nationalist/Islamist press) as evidence of state/MİT ties, or an internal contradiction worth noting. ⚪ = no such reading found.

## A. Chronological entries

### 1. 2000–2001 — ECHR defence: *Sümer Rahip Devletinden Demokratik Uygarlığa* (AİHM Savunmaları, 2 cilt)
- **Context:** Written in İmralı for the ECtHR application (Öcalan v. Turkey). Vol. I published Nov 2001 (Mezopotamya Yayınları, Köln). Introduces "demokratik uygarlık" and "demokratik cumhuriyet" theses; abandons the demand for an independent state.
- **Quote:** [PARAPHRASE] Öcalan stresses that Kurds and Turks are "kardeş" and insists these thoughts are not pragmatic manoeuvres produced by prison conditions.
- **Source:** https://books.google.com/books/about/S%C3%BCmer_rahip_devletinden_demokratik_uyga.html?id=_erFzQEACAAJ ; PDF copy: https://birinsanbirkitap.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/08/sumer-rahip-devletinden-demokratik-uygarliga-cilt11.pdf
- **Reliability:** A (book text) — but the specific "not pragmatic" line is a snippet paraphrase.
- **Flag:** 🚩 Critics (Sakık, Okçuoğlu, nationalist press) point to the abrupt 1999–2001 shift from "bağımsız Kürdistan" to "demokratik cumhuriyet / Türk-Kürt kardeşliği" as evidence of a deal with the state. Counter: the shift is consistent with his 1999 trial defence and with a captured leader seeking to avoid execution.

### 2. 4 April 2002 — PKK → KADEK
- **Context:** PKK 8th Congress (Jan–Apr 2002) declares "PKK adıyla yürütülen faaliyetlerin durdurulduğu" and founds KADEK (Kürdistan Özgürlük ve Demokrasi Kongresi), following Öcalan's İmralı directives conveyed via lawyers.
- **Quote:** [PARAPHRASE] The 4 April 2002 declaration says all activity under the PKK name is halted.
- **Source:** https://t24.com.tr/gundem/pkk-neden-kuruldu-bugune-nasil-geldi,1249208 ; https://tudpam.org/degisim-degil-donusum-pkknin-kck-yapilanmasi/
- **Reliability:** B.
- **Flag:** ⚪ (Turkish state read it as cosmetic; not usually cited as MİT evidence).

### 3. 27 Oct–6 Nov 2003 — KONGRA-GEL founding congress, Kandil
- **Context:** Congress line set by "Öcalan'ın yazdığı rapor"; congress decides to invite two of Öcalan's lawyers.
- **Quote:** none verbatim retrieved.
- **Source:** https://tudpam.org/degisim-degil-donusum-pkknin-kck-yapilanmasi/
- **Reliability:** B.
- **Flag:** 🚩 The presence of İmralı lawyers (Mahmut Şakar, İrfan Dündar) at Kandil in 2003–04 is the hinge of Okçuoğlu's and Sakık's later claim that "Ergenekon" messages reached Kandil through lawyers (see Section B).

### 4. Feb–Sept 2004 — Osman Öcalan / PWD split
- **Context:** Faction (Osman Öcalan, Nizamettin Taş, Kani Yılmaz, Hıdır Sarıkaya) breaks away, wants to abandon armed struggle, work with AKP, USA, KDP. Founds PWD (Partiya Welatparêzên Demokrat), Sept 2004. Abdullah Öcalan distances himself and later publicly excludes them.
- **Quote (Öcalan):** none verbatim retrieved this session. [UNVERIFIED THIS SESSION] Lawyer notes of spring 2004 have him calling the dissidents "tasfiyeci" and linking them to "ABD".
- **Source:** https://medyascope.tv/2021/03/13/gomasinen-31-osman-ocalan-ve-arkadaslarinin-orgutu-pwdnin-kurulus-ve-cokus-oykusu-eylul-2004/
- **Reliability:** B.
- **Flag:** 🚩 Two competing accusations: PWD accused Abdullah/Kandil of sabotaging peace; Okçuoğlu (2009) says the *anti*-ceasefire message of May 2004 came from Öcalan via Mahmut Şakar at Ergenekon's behest.

### 5. May–1 June 2004 — end of the 1999 unilateral ceasefire
- **Context:** Sakık (then already in Turkish custody) and Okçuoğlu both claim that in May 2004 lawyers Mahmut Şakar and İrfan Dündar came to camps "claiming to speak on behalf of the leadership"; the ceasefire ended 1 June 2004.
- **Quote (Sakık):** [PARAPHRASE] Mahmut Şakar and İrfan Dündar came to PKK camps in May 2004 claiming to speak for the leadership.
- **Source:** https://t24.com.tr/gundem/mit-ocalani-ajan-olarak-kullandi-mi,68997 ; https://www.yenisafak.com/gundem/ergenekon-darbeye-hazirlanirken-apoya-ateskesi-kaldirtti-276316
- **Reliability:** C (both claimants hostile to Öcalan; Sakık was an ex-PKK defector cooperating with Turkish prosecutors).
- **Flag:** 🚩 Central to the "Ergenekon used Öcalan" narrative. Counter-evidence: KONGRA-GEL had already publicly discussed ending the ceasefire in early 2004 over the absence of state reciprocity; no documentary trace of a Şakar–Ergenekon link surfaced in the Ergenekon indictments beyond hearsay.

### 8. Pre-2005 (exact date unpublished) — the single Emre Taner meeting in İmralı
- **Context:** MİT operations deputy undersecretary Emre Taner met Öcalan once in İmralı "devletin bilgisi dahilinde", records archived. Later, as MİT chief (2005–10), Taner's request for a second meeting was blocked by the Bursa garrison commander (per Mehmet Ali Şahin, Justice Minister 2007–09).
- **Quote (Öcalan on Taner):** [VERBATIM – snippet, Turkish original not retrieved; English gloss as reported] "I didn't know Taner. He was a very mature person, I was surprised. They were saying 'Let's solve this problem with you, not with the KDP, the PUK and America'."
- **Source:** https://www.evrensel.net/haber/167646/mit-ocalan-la-gorustu ; https://www.yenisafak.com/gundem/taner-ocalanla-devletin-bilgisi-dahilinde-gorusmus-144390 ; https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/gundem/emre-taner-ocalanla-gorusmek-istedi-ama-22346882
- **Reliability:** B (Öcalan's account via lawyer notes; state side confirmed by Şahin and by Yeni Şafak reporting).
- **Flag:** 🚩 Öcalan himself volunteers that MİT proposed "solving the problem with you". Critics read it as proof of a standing channel; supporters read it as the state courting him. Both readings agree a channel existed.

### 10. July 2008 — lawyer notes on the Ergenekon arrests (Tolon, Eruygur)
- **Context:** After 1–7 July 2008 arrests (retired Gens. Hurşit Tolon, Şener Eruygur), Öcalan's weekly notes discuss Ergenekon.
- **Quotes:** [VERBATIM – snippet, English as reported; Turkish original not retrieved] Öcalan said "Ergenekon's connections to DHKP-C, İBDA-C, Hizbullah, and PKK are being discussed", claimed retired Gen. Hurşit Tolon and several commanders "had previously met with him" and that "Hüseyin Kıvrıkoğlu's men had also met with him"; and: "They are linking me and the PKK to Ergenekon… They are trying to tarnish my name."
- **Source:** http://ankaderin.blogspot.com/2008/07/ergenekoncular-apoyla-grsmsler.html (blog reproducing ANF notes) ; https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ergenekon_soru%C5%9Fturmas%C4%B1_kronolojisi
- **Reliability:** C (blog copy of ANF lawyer notes; wording via snippet).
- **Flag:** 🚩 This is the closest documented origin of the "Ergenekon met Öcalan" line — note it is *Öcalan himself* saying generals met him (as garrison/İmralı commanders), while simultaneously denying any organisational tie. The popular formula "Ergenekon beni kullandı" was **not** found verbatim in any source this session; treat it as a paraphrase circulating in commentary (e.g., soL, Merdan Yanardağ "Öcalan'dan Ergenekon özeleştirisi", https://haber.sol.org.tr/yazarlar/merdan-yanardag/ocalan-dan-ergenekon-ozelestirisi-38026).

### 11. 2009 (spring) — Okçuoğlu's public accusation
- **Context:** Former lawyer Ahmet Zeki Okçuoğlu (dismissed by Öcalan in 1999) tells press Öcalan told him he had contact with the "derin devlet".
- **Quote (Okçuoğlu on Öcalan):** [PARAPHRASE] Öcalan told him he was in contact with the deep state and "couldn't stop praising" it; PKK–Ergenekon relations ran through lawyer Mahmut Şakar; Şakar went to Kandil in 2004 as Öcalan's representative and "ordered on behalf of Öcalan" that the disarmament decision be invalidated; Öcalan also met retired Gen. Çevik Bir.
- **Source:** https://www.yenisafak.com/gundem/ergenekon-darbeye-hazirlanirken-apoya-ateskesi-kaldirtti-276316 ; https://bianet.org/yazi/ateskes-ozal-in-33-askerin-oldurulmesi-devletin-projesiydi-112123
- **Reliability:** C — single hostile source; Yeni Şafak (pro-AKP, then anti-Ergenekon) had motive to amplify.
- **Flag:** 🚩 Primary source of the "Öcalan–Ergenekon" allegation. Counter-evidence: Okçuoğlu's claim of a Çevik Bir meeting is unsupported by prison logs made public; the Ergenekon indictments never charged Öcalan or Şakar.

### 12. Aug 2009 — "Yol Haritası" (Demokratik Anayasal Çözüm / Roadmap)
- **Context:** ~160-page text written for the state during the "Demokratik Açılım"; a 20-page summary letter sent to BDP via the Justice Ministry; confiscated, released only in 2011 by ECtHR route. Sections: "Barış Felsefesi", "Eylem Planı", "Karşılaşılacak Sorunlar ve Sonuç". Demands: constitutional status for Kurds, mother-tongue education, threshold removal, European Charter of Local Self-Government, abolition of terror laws, truth & reconciliation commission.
- **Quote:** [VERBATIM – snippet, title only] "Yol Haritama Güveniyorum" (headline of lawyer-note report).
- **Source:** https://bianet.org/bianet/siyaset/117043-ocalan-yol-haritama-guveniyorum ; https://www.voaturkce.com/a/ocalandan-yol-haritasi/1611667.html
- **Reliability:** A/B.
- **Flag:** 🚩 Written *at the state's request* (Öcalan says the state asked for it). Nationalist critics: "the state is negotiating with a terrorist"; PKK dissidents: proof of coordination. Neither is evidence of agency; it is evidence of dialogue, which the government itself confirmed in 2012.

- **Quote:** [VERBATIM – headline] "Benimle Devlet Görüşüyor, Nereden Geldiklerinin Önemi Yok" ("The state is talking to me; where they come from doesn't matter"). [PARAPHRASE]
- **Source:** https://bianet.org/haber/ocalan-benimle-devlet-gorusuyor-nereden-geldiklerinin-onemi-yok-124433 ; https://bianet.org/haber/oslo-gorusmeleri-nedir-140937

### 18. 17 Nov 2012 — call to end the prison hunger strikes (via Mehmet Öcalan)
- **Context:** 67th day of hunger strikes by ~700 prisoners demanding Öcalan's freedom/mother-tongue rights. Mehmet Öcalan visits İmralı and relays the call; strikes end that evening.
- **Quote:** [PARAPHRASE, close] The strikers had "taken on tasks that should belong to those outside"; hunger strikes are generally a problematic tactic and if done should be done by people outside, not inside; the action "has found its place and achieved its purpose" ("amacına ulaşmıştır") and should be ended "without hesitation".
- **Source:** https://www.voaturkce.com/a/1548441.html ; https://www.cnnturk.com/2012/turkiye/11/18/aclik.grevleri.sona.erdi/685028.0/index.html ; https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/turkiye/aclik-grevini-bitirin-cagrisi/308190
- **Reliability:** B (second-hand via brother; no written text).
- **Flag:** 🚩 Timing: the call came one day after Erdoğan's 16 Nov 2012 message that the state had "reached out" to İmralı; critics said the government "switched Apo on" to end the strike. Counter: the strike had reached lethal stage and Kurdish politicians had been asking for exactly this call.

### 21. 21 March 2013 — Newroz letter, Diyarbakır (read by Pervin Buldan in Kurdish, Sırrı Süreyya Önder in Turkish)
- **Context:** The founding text of the Çözüm Süreci.
- **Quotes:** [VERBATIM – snippet]
  - "Artık silahlar sussun, fikirler ve siyasetler konuşsun." — *Let the guns fall silent; let ideas and politics speak.*
  - "Ben, bu çağrıma kulak veren milyonların şahitliğinde diyorum ki; artık yeni bir dönem başlıyor, silah değil, siyaset öne çıkıyor." — *I say before the millions who heed this call: a new era is beginning; not arms but politics comes to the fore.*
  - "Artık silahlı unsurlarımızın sınır ötesine çekilmesi aşamasına gelinmiştir." — *The stage has come for our armed elements to withdraw beyond the border.*
  - "Bu bir son değil, yeni bir başlangıçtır." — *This is not an end but a new beginning.*
  - "Bu mücadeleyi bırakma değil, daha farklı bir mücadeleyi başlatmadır." — *This is not abandoning the struggle but starting a different struggle.*
  - [PARAPHRASE, confirmed themes] Frames the moment as a contemporary "derivative" of the Milli Kurtuluş Savaşı fought "Misak-ı Milli çerçevesinde Türklerin ve Kürtlerin öncülüğünde"; calls for a "Milli Dayanışma ve Barış Konferansı" uniting Kurds, Turkmens, Assyrians and Arabs "Misak-ı Milli'ye aykırı olarak parçalanmış" (divided contrary to the National Pact); references to "Anadolu ve Mezopotamya" and to peoples who have "lived under the flag of Islam for a thousand years" (that phrase widely reported but not retrieved verbatim here).
- **Source:** https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/haber/iste-ocalanin-mesaji-410678 ; https://t24.com.tr/haber/iste-ocalanin-tarihi-newroz-mektubunun-turkce-tam-metni,291107 ; https://sendika.org/2013/03/abdullah-ocalanin-newroz-mesajinin-tam-metni-bu-son-degil-yeni-bir-baslangictir-98776 ; https://www.aljazeera.com.tr/haber/ocalanin-2013teki-mektubu
- **Reliability:** A.
- **Flag:** 🚩 Two contradictory suspicions: (i) Turkish nationalists/Kemalists: text was "written with MİT" — the Misak-ı Milli/İslam framing sounds like AKP language; (ii) Kurdish nationalists/dissidents (PWD, Burkay, later PAK): Öcalan "sold" Kurdish national demands for Turkish-Islamic unity. Counter: the Misak-ı Milli theme already appears in his 2009 Roadmap and 2001 ECHR defence.

**Overall assessment for 2000–2023.** Every documented Öcalan statement about the state in this period describes *negotiation* (Taner ≤2005; Fidan/MİT 2009–11; Fidan/Akdoğan 2013–15) that the Turkish government itself has officially acknowledged (Erdoğan, 28 Dec 2012; Fidan on the Oslo tape). No document, testimony under oath, or intercept produced in 2000–2023 shows Öcalan taking instructions from MİT. The strongest "state-use" episodes are one-directional: the state releasing/timing his texts (Nov 2012, June 2019) or using relatives (Osman on TRT). The strongest "contradiction" episodes are Öcalan's shifting self-descriptions of who met him (generals in 2008; "nereden geldiklerinin önemi yok" in 2010) — consistent with an isolated prisoner inferring the identity of his visitors rather than with a handled asset.
