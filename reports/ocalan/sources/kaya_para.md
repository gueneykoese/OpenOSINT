# "Pilot" Necati Kaya — where did the money come from?

Working notes, 2026-09-08. Method: web search only (Turkish/Kurdish/English). **Direct page fetching was blocked by network policy**, so every item below rests on search-engine snippets, not full-text reads. Quotes are reproduced as they appeared in snippets; wording may be abridged by the engine. Anything I could not confirm is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

Reliability grades: **A** primary/official or contemporaneous document; **B** named journalist/participant on the record, consistent across outlets; **C** secondary retelling, single source, or partisan outlet; **D** anonymous, hearsay, or internally contradictory.

## 2. Hypotheses for the money

### H1 — Legitimate wage: THK/Türkkuşu pilot salary (1977), later commercial/crop-dusting pay
**For:** Öcalan himself uses the word "maaş"; Türkkuşu employment 1977 is documented (tasking baseline, Pekmezci documents). Emekli Sandığı membership to 1976 (see §4) suggests a payroll job with pension deductions. Ant Birlik employment 1982 is certain (crash).
**Against:** A 20–21-year-old sport-aviation pilot's salary cannot plausibly fund restaurants for a dozen people, house rent for meetings, travel, and (allegedly) weapons — see §3. Öcalan's "avuç avuç" language is incompatible with a junior THK wage.
**Grade of support:** B for the job; C for it being the main source.

### H2 — Family money / land sales (Ağrı)
**For:** The only first-person explanation attributed to Kaya: "ailesinden para var... arazi sattım" (T24 quoting Sayın 1996). Kin/"aile dostu" link to the Yıldırım family and Polat-aşiret introducer point to a landed Tutak/Ağrı milieu. Land sales by eastern aşiret families to fund a son in Ankara were common.
**Against:** No independent record of the Kaya family's holdings, of Ferzende Kaya, or of any sale surfaced. Öcalan reports it only to doubt it. No relative has ever spoken publicly.
**Grade:** C. Plausible but unevidenced.

### H3 — Smuggling / grey trade (Ağrı–Iran border; Diyarbakır "kum ticareti")
**For:** Yurtçiçek/Aydınlık's sand-trade line; Ağrı border families often had trade income.
**Against:** Sand-trade story is age-incompatible and single-sourced; no smuggling allegation naming Kaya or his family was found in any language.
**Grade:** D. Effectively unsupported; listed because the tasking asked.

### H4 — State money (MİT / Özel Harp / "örtülü ödenek")
**For:** Öcalan's repeated post-1979 assertions; 1979 Tekoşin statement calling him MİT; Mumcu's synthesis; son's "sivil istihbarat" remark; Ergenekon-era "10 milyon" document.
**Against:** Every source traces back to (i) Öcalan's own retrospective, self-serving narrative, (ii) a hostile splinter group in a purge atmosphere, or (iii) an anonymous 2000 letter containing an impossible detail. No pay record, no MİT personnel record, no bank trace has ever been produced, including by Pekmezci who had the family's papers. Öcalan's "örtülü ödenek" line is a conjecture, not knowledge. The son's remark is suggestive but not evidence of payment.
**Grade:** C for "Kaya had some intelligence relationship"; D for "the money was state money".

### H5 — Another service (CIA etc.)
**For:** Baki Tuğ's "CIA/MİT" phrasing.
**Against:** Tuğ's account is a conflation with İlyas Aydın; nothing else.
**Grade:** D.

### H6 — Exaggeration by Öcalan (and by later press)
**For:** Öcalan's motive to retro-brand early patrons as agents after the 1979–80 purges and after Kesire's defection (1987–88); the escalation from "maaş"/"arazi sattı" (1996) to "çok zengin, kontrgerilla" (1988 Birand) to press "en ciddi finans kaynağı" (2011); numbers inflate over time (one year → five years; meals → weapons → 10 million). The concrete first-hand items are modest: meals, a flat used for a 3-day meeting, some cash.
**Against:** Even sceptical contemporaries (Tekoşin 1979) thought Pilot's spending was anomalous.
**Grade:** B as a partial explanation. Best-supported single reading: a real but modest patron whose importance was later inflated by all sides.

## 3. Scale check: a 1977 Türkkuşu pilot's pay vs "feeding the group at restaurants"

Reference points (all from snippets; **no THK pay table found**):
- Civil-service coefficient (katsayı): 12 in 1977, 14 in 1978 (DergiPark article on 1979 budget, https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/281352). Grade B.
- 1976 lise teacher ≈ 5,546 TL/month; ilkokul teacher ≈ 4,750 TL/month (memurlar.net, https://www.memurlar.net/haber/14607/ogretmen-maasi-ile-29-yil-once-141-kilo-et-alinabiliyordu.html). Grade B.
- A junior memur starting Feb 1977 recalls "900 lira" (https://www.memurlar.net/haber/8958/1977-yilinda-verilen-900-liralik-maasimi-istiyorum.html). Grade C (single anecdote, possibly a partial/first-month figure).
- Minimum wage 1977 ≈ 1,800–1,820 TL/month (vergidosyasi.com; ihybagimsiz PDF). Grade B.
- Beef ≈ 39 TL/kg in 1976 (derived: 5,546 TL / 141 kg). Grade B.

Estimate for a 21-year-old Türkkuşu pilot (THK was a kamu yararına dernek paying near-public scales): probably **3,000–6,000 TL/month** in 1977. UNVERIFIED; bracketed between a new memur and a senior teacher.

Cost of restaurant hospitality (no 1977 lokanta menu found; **estimate only**): a working-class Ankara lokanta meal in 1977 ≈ 15–30 TL/person (consistent with beef at ~39 TL/kg). Feeding 10–15 people once ≈ 200–400 TL. Doing so 2–3 times a week ≈ 2,000–5,000 TL/month.

Conclusion of the arithmetic: **occasional group meals are within a Türkkuşu salary; habitual meals plus rent, travel and any arms purchases are not.** If Öcalan's description is accurate, a second source is required — which is exactly the gap that "family/land", "MİT" and "exaggeration" each try to fill. The arithmetic cannot distinguish between those three.

The "10 milyon TL" figure is, by comparison, ~1,800–2,000 teacher-months of salary in 1977 terms; nothing in the group's documented lifestyle (student flats, borrowed money, Fis village) reflects money on that scale. It should be treated as a fabrication or a later-lira anachronism.

## 4. The Emekli Sandığı question

Tasking says Kaya was an Emekli Sandığı member until 1976. Does that imply a public-sector job?
- Emekli Sandığı (Law 5434) covered state employees and, by explicit listing in Art. 12, certain public-benefit bodies' staff. My recollection is that **Türk Hava Kurumu personnel were among the listed iştirakçi categories (together with Kızılay and Çocuk Esirgeme Kurumu)**, but I could not confirm this from snippets — the search returned only the law's general text (https://www.lexpera.com.tr/resmi-gazete/metin/turkiye-cumhuriyeti-emekli-sandigi-kanunu-7235-5434 ; https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.3.5434.pdf). **UNVERIFIED — Grade C.** Someone with page access should check 5434 md. 12/II for "Türk Hava Kurumu".
- If THK staff were iştirakçi, membership "until 1976" is fully explained by a THK job or paid trainee status ending/changing in 1976, and says nothing about MİT. If THK staff were *not* iştirakçi, then an Emekli Sandığı record for a 18–20-year-old implies some other state employer — which would be a genuinely interesting lead. Note that MİT personnel were themselves Emekli Sandığı members, so the record cannot exclude that either; but the simplest reading is THK.
- Membership ending in 1976 followed by "Türkkuşu pilot 1977" is odd if both were THK — possibly a shift from salaried staff to contract/per-flight pay, or a data-entry artefact. UNVERIFIED.

## 5. Ant Birlik 1982
Ant Birlik (Antalya Pamuk ve Narenciye Tarım Satış Kooperatifleri Birliği) operated its own crop-dusting fleet ("Antbirlik'e bağlı ilaçlama filosu"). Kaya was flying for it on 9 Sept 1982 when the aircraft struck HV lines near Niğip, Manavgat, and burned (Milliyet 10 Sept 1982 via https://www.milliyet.com.tr/gundem/iste-ocalanin-can-simidi-pilot-necati-1376058). No fleet, contract, or pay information for 1982 surfaced. Crop-dusting was seasonal contract work with pay per hectare/flight hour — modest, not "avuç avuç" money. That a supposed well-funded state asset was doing dangerous seasonal agricultural flying in 1982 is itself mild evidence against a lavish state stipend (or evidence it had ended). Grade C inference.

## 6. Final assessment

1. **What is actually documented** about Kaya's income: a THK/Türkkuşu affiliation (1974–77), a pension-fund record to 1976, a commercial licence, and an Ant Birlik crop-dusting job at death. All are ordinary, modest aviation wages.
2. **What Kaya himself said** (per Öcalan): family money, pilot earnings, land sold. This is the only first-person explanation and is unrefuted, though unproven.
3. **What Öcalan alleged** (1979 onward): that the money was really MİT/Özel Harp/"örtülü ödenek". This is conjecture by an interested party, repeated by Mumcu and the press without independent documentation. The son's later "sivil istihbarat" hint keeps the possibility alive but adds no evidence of payment.
4. **The specific figure of 10 million lira** is worthless as evidence (anonymous, fourth-hand, contains an impossible detail).
5. **Baki Tuğ's account** conflates Kaya with İlyas Aydın; the "Diyarbakır sand trade / ex-army" line is age-incompatible with a 1956 birth. Both should be discarded unless the birth date itself is shown wrong.
6. **Most probable picture**, on present evidence: a young pilot from a landed Ağrı family with modest wage income plus some family/land money, generous with a student circle for a year or so in 1976–77; his generosity was retrospectively magnified by Öcalan (to explain away early patrons after the purges), by Aydınlık (to discredit the PKK), and by 2000s Ergenekon-era press. Whether *any* of the money was state money remains **open and unverifiable** from open sources; no document has ever been produced, including by the journalist who held the family's papers.

Open leads for someone with page access: (i) full text of *Erkeği Öldürmek* pp. ~230–240 (issuu) for the exact "örtülü ödenek" sentence; (ii) Law 5434 Art. 12 for THK staff; (iii) Pekmezci's reproduced documents (any pay slip, Emekli Sandığı sicil, tapu); (iv) Aydınlık 27 June–2 Sept 1979 originals; (v) Milliyet 10 Sept 1982 crash report; (vi) Ağrı/Tutak tapu or aşiret sources on the Kaya family of Hizyan.
