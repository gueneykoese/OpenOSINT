# Part 2 — Abdullah Öcalan's own statements, 1990 → 15 Feb 1999 (Nairobi) + the 1999 İmralı trial

**Compiled:** 2026-09-06. **Method:** WebSearch (TR/EN/KU queries) — note that in this session the egress proxy blocked *all* WebFetch retrievals (Yeni Şafak archive, Milliyet, Hürriyet, T24, bianet, Serbestiyet, tr.wikipedia / Vikikaynak, ANF, Rudaw, archive.org, etc.), so no primary page could be read in full. Quotes below therefore come from search-engine snippets and summaries of the linked pages. Each entry carries a **Verification** grade:

- **V** = the Turkish wording appears verbatim in a snippet of the cited page (still: re-check against the page before publication).
- **P** = paraphrase / reported speech from a secondary source; wording not confirmed.
- **U** = widely-circulated attribution that I could not confirm in any source reached; treat as unverified.

**Suspicion flag** = (a) contradicts an earlier/later Öcalan statement, or (b) has been cited by others as evidence of ties to the Turkish state/MİT. Flags are analytical observations, not conclusions.

## A. Chronological entries

### 1. Late 1980s–1990 — Meetings with Doğu Perinçek (October 1989, Beqaa/Damascus) → published in *2000'e Doğru*
- **Context:** Perinçek, then editor of *2000'e Doğru*, visited Öcalan in October 1989 and again in April 1991 (during the Gulf War). Interviews were published as *Abdullah Öcalan ile Görüşmeler* (first five printings 1990–91, sold out; later reissued by Kaynak).
- **Quote:** No verbatim Öcalan line retrievable in this session. Perinçek's later framing: that "almost all leading journalists — Cıvaoğlu, Altaylı, Hasan Cemal, Birand, Çandar — also met Apo." (**P**)
- **Sources:** https://www.goodreads.com/tr/book/show/26115559-abdullah-calan-ile-g-r-meler ; https://vatanpartisi.org.tr/genel-merkez/soru-yanit/dogu-perincek-abdullah-ocalan-la-neden-gorustu-10876 ; http://www.haber7.com/siyaset/haber/249124-perincek-ocalani-ziyaretinden-pisman-degil ; https://www.yenisafak.com/gundem/ocalan-perincek-kucaklasmasinin-hikayesi-421364
- **Reliability:** Dates of visits (Oct 1989, Apr 1991) consistent across Vatan Partisi's own account and hostile sources (Yeni Şafak, SDE).
- **Suspicion flag:** (b) — The Öcalan–Perinçek embrace is routinely cited (e.g. by Islamist/AKP-aligned outlets and later by Kurdish critics) as evidence of Öcalan's alignment with "Kemalist/Ergenekon" circles; Can Dündar in 2012 said "Perinçek, Öcalan'a yol gösterirdi" (https://www.sabah.com.tr/gundem/2012/04/18/dundar-perincek-ocalana-yol-gosterirdi). Counter: an interview is not a control relationship; Perinçek's group published the material openly and was prosecuted for it.

### 2. February 1991 — Second Birand interview (Damascus)
- **Context:** Birand first met Öcalan in 1988 (Beqaa); the second interview took place February 1991 in Damascus. Both were compiled in *Apo ve PKK* (1992), which was banned; Milliyet was confiscated and Birand tried at DGM (acquitted), then prosecuted over the book.
- **Quote (reported lead of Milliyet's original report):** "PKK lideri silahla hiçbir şeyin elde edilemeyeceğini ve ateşkese hazır olduğunu söyledi." — *"The PKK leader said nothing can be achieved by arms and he is ready for a ceasefire."* (**P** — this is the newspaper's summary sentence, not Öcalan's words.)
- **Sources:** https://medyagunlugu.com/birandin-basini-agritan-roportaj/ ; https://eksiseyler.com/mehmet-ali-birandin-hapisle-yargilanmasina-neden-olan-ocalan-roportajlari ; https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/3037787-apo-ve-pkk ; https://www.karar.com/yazarlar/yildiray-ogur/bir-roportajin-tarihi-1605647
- **Reliability:** Medium; secondary retellings. The 1988 date for the first interview is firm.
- **Suspicion flag:** (a) — a 1991 "ready for ceasefire / arms achieve nothing" line sits uneasily with the PKK's 1991–93 escalation (Serhildan period) and with 1994 "people's war" rhetoric (entry 12). Also (b): Sakık's forged 1998 "andıç" listed Birand among journalists "paid by PKK" (see entry 30), which later turned out fabricated by Çevik Bir/Özkasnak.

- **Quote:** none retrievable (**U**).

### 4. ~1990–93 — Yalçın Küçük interviews (Beqaa / Damascus) → *Kürt Bahçesinde Sözleşi* (Başak Yayınları, 1993)
- **Context:** Küçük conducted extended conversations; book banned for a period. Küçük framed his role as "mediation" and the text as "a kind of peace covenant between the Turkish and Kurdish peoples". Sources disagree whether the core sessions were 1990–91 or 1993; the published book is 1993 and the search summaries date the meeting to 1993.
- **Quote:** No verbatim Öcalan line retrievable (**U**). Full text is on Scribd/Academia (not reachable here): https://www.academia.edu/7774053/ ; https://www.scribd.com/document/651656987/
- **Sources:** https://politikaakademisi.org/2016/10/11/prof-dr-yalcin-kucukten-kurt-bahcesinde-sozlesi/ ; https://1000kitap.com/kitap/kurt-bahcesinde-sozlesi--199260
- **Reliability:** Existence and 1993 publication firm; date of sessions uncertain.
- **Suspicion flag:** (b) — Küçük later became an Ergenekon defendant; hostile Islamist outlets (İLKHA, Doğruhaber) call him "karanlık yapıların adamı" and treat the Öcalan sessions as evidence of a "deep" PKK–state channel (https://ilkha.com/gundem/karanlik-yapilarin-adami-yalcin-kucuk-oldu-523041). Counter-evidence: Küçük was himself prosecuted for the book; being interviewed by a Kemalist-left intellectual is not evidence of MİT direction.

### 5. May 1990 — İsmail Beşikçi on Öcalan (context, not an Öcalan statement)
- **Context:** Beşikçi wrote "Bütün Apolara selam" and praised "PKK Genel Sekreteri Abdullah Öcalan'ın bilim adamı yönü". After 1999 he broke with Öcalan over the "accommodationist" line.
- **Sources:** https://eksisozluk.com/ismail-besikci--53871 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%B0smail_Be%C5%9Fik%C3%A7i
- **Reliability:** P.
- **Suspicion flag:** none for this period; Beşikçi's later criticism is that Öcalan abandoned independence, not that he was an agent.

### 6. 1992–93 — Oral Çalışlar interviews (Beqaa), published in *Cumhuriyet* over 15 days
- **Context:** Topics ranged from daily politics to Alevism, women, homosexuality, Islam. Çalışlar was later sentenced to 13 months for the book version.
- **Quote (on Özal's death, by phone, ~17–18 Apr 1993):** "Osmanlı'da oyun çoktur. Onu zehirlemişlerdir." — *"There are many intrigues in the Ottoman [tradition]. They must have poisoned him."* (**V** — in Serbestiyet's tweet/interview text.)
- **Sources:** https://serbestiyet.com/roportaj/oral-calislar-anlatiyor-93te-ocalana-ozal-oldu-deyince-bana-ne-demisti-193107/ ; https://x.com/serbestiyetweb/status/1876935023724560619
- **Reliability:** First-hand recollection by the interlocutor, 30 years later.
- **Suspicion flag:** (a) — Öcalan's private "they poisoned him" (1993) vs. his 8 June 1993 public line that Özal "conducted the most comprehensive war against us" (entry 10) vs. his 2025 letter calling Özal a "demokrasi şehidi" (Rudaw, entry 10 note). Also Çalışlar's separate recollection that in 1993 Öcalan "mistook a journalist who came before us for a MİT envoy and got his hopes up; was very upset when he learned the truth" (https://serbestiyet.com/featured/oral-calislar-ocalan-93te-bizden-once-gorustugu-gazeteciyi-mitten-sanip-umutlanmisti-gercegi-ogrenince-cok-uzuldu-208925/) — cited both ways: proof he *wanted* a state channel, and proof he did not *have* one.

### 7. 17 March 1993 — Bar Elias (Beqaa) press conference: first unilateral ceasefire, with Celal Talabani and Ahmet Türk
- **Context:** Announced ceasefire for 20 March–15 April 1993. Talabani shuttled between Ankara and Damascus; Cengiz Çandar acted as go-between to President Özal. Kemal Burkay, Hemreş Reşo, Ruşen Aslan came to Bar Elias in support.
- **Quote (20 March 1993 formulation reported in Vikipedi "Abdullah Öcalan'ın sorgulanması"):** "Ordu birlikleri üzerimize gelmedikçe ve çok zorunlu bir meşru savunma durumuna düşmedikçe 20 Mart–15 Nisan arasında ateş etmeyeceğiz." — *"Unless army units come at us and unless we fall into a strictly necessary situation of legitimate self-defence, we will not fire between 20 March and 15 April."* (**P/V** — snippet paraphrase of the Vikipedi rendering; original wording to be checked against video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rsiebMODtAw)
- **Sources:** https://bianet.org/yazi/ateskes-ozal-in-33-askerin-oldurulmesi-devletin-projesiydi-112123 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Kurdistan_Workers%27_Party_ceasefire ; https://yeniyasamgazetesi9.com/cozum-arayislarinin-donum-noktalari-1/ ; https://ilketv.com.tr/1993ten-gunumuze-kurt-meselesi-ve-cozumu-1-1993-ateskesi-ozal-projesi/
- **Reliability:** High for event/date; medium for wording.
- **Suspicion flag:** (b) — Ülkücü/nationalist commentators (Hakkı Öznur) cite the Özal–Öcalan channel as proof that "the state started talks with Apo" (https://enpolitik.com/haber/...355741); PKK-critical Kurds note the ceasefire was effectively an Özal project ("Ateşkes Özal'ın… projesiydi", bianet). Neither shows MİT *control*; both show a negotiated channel existed.

### 10. 8 June 1993 — Beqaa press conference: ceasefire declared over
- **Context:** After Bingöl (24 May) and Özal's death; Öcalan blamed Demirel and İnönü for "misusing" the process.
- **Quotes (reported by İlke TV series):** 
  - "Sayın Özal'ın 'PKK biraz yumuşayamaz mı?' mesajı ışığında ateşkes durumunu biraz geliştirdik." — *"We developed the ceasefire somewhat in light of Mr Özal's message asking 'can't the PKK soften a bit?'"* (**P/V**)
  - On Özal: "En kapsamlı savaşı bizimle o yürüttü; diplomatik, ekonomik, hatta siyasi taktik cephesini o yönetti." — *"He conducted the most comprehensive war against us; he managed the diplomatic, economic, even political-tactical front."* (**P**)
  - "Ateşkesin hedefi biraz Özal'dı, gelişme düzeyi Çankaya'ydı." (**P**)
- **Sources:** https://ilketv.com.tr/ateskesin-aciklanmasindan-2-gun-sonra-ozalin-olumu/ ; https://ilketv.com.tr/ciller-donemi-ya-bitecek-ya-bitecek/
- **Reliability:** Medium (İlke TV is a Kurdish-movement-aligned outlet reconstructing from PKK archives).
- **Suspicion flag:** (a) — 2025 letter to Ahmet Özal calls Turgut Özal a "demokrasi şehidi" (https://www.rudaw.net/turkish/middleeast/turkey/18062025 ; https://www.sozcu.com.tr/ocalan-dan-turgut-ozal-in-ogluna-dikkat-ceken-mektup-p185135) — a re-framing of the same man from "war-manager" to martyr.

### 11. 24 May 1993 — Bingöl (Elazığ–Bingöl highway): 33 unarmed soldiers killed by Şemdin Sakık's unit — Öcalan's conflicting statements
- **Context:** Ceasefire still nominally in force. Three versions of responsibility:
  1. **Öcalan claimed it (1993):** Sakık's book asserts Öcalan "later claimed responsibility in a BBC Radio interview and called it retaliation (misilleme), then backtracked." (**P** — from tr.wikipedia summary of *İmralı'da Bir Tiran*; I could not locate the BBC audio/transcript.)
  2. **Öcalan denied ordering it / blamed Sakık:** PKK leadership at Kandil and Öcalan blamed Sakık; Sakık "kept denying" in prison. Öcalan in a lawyer meeting (İmralı, date unspecified): "33 askeri ateşkesi bozmak için bize öldürttüler." — *"They had us kill the 33 soldiers to break the ceasefire."* (**P** — bianet.)
  3. **Sakık's later version (≈2004 and 2011–12 testimony):** in early May 1993 Öcalan, by radio from Lebanon, ordered a retaliatory action after a Turkish officer was "disrespectful" on the radio; Sakık executed it. Sakık's *first* statement after capture (1998) said nothing about an Öcalan order.
  - Court finding (Sakık trial): "sanığın olaya katıldığı, planladığı veya emir verdiği tespit edilememiştir; ancak olay sanığın sorumluluk alanında gerçekleşmiştir, dolaylı sorumluluğu vardır." (**P**)
  - A survivor (T24, 2012): "'Vurun' emrini Sakık verdi." (**V** headline.)
- **Sources:** https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/1993_Bing%C3%B6l_sald%C4%B1r%C4%B1s%C4%B1 ; https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C4%B0mral%C4%B1'da_Bir_Tiran:_Abdullah_%C3%96calan ; https://t24.com.tr/gundem/bingol-baskinindan-kurtulan-asker-vurun-emrini-sakik-verdi,216985 ; https://www.takvim.com.tr/guncel/2011/11/03/33-askerin-olum-emri-ocalandan ; https://bianet.org/yazi/ateskes-ozal-in-33-askerin-oldurulmesi-devletin-projesiydi-112123 ; https://bianet.org/haber/33-asker-kurtulabilirdi-137579 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_24,_1993,_PKK_attack
- **Reliability:** Low-to-medium on *who ordered*: Sakık is a hostile, self-interested witness whose story changed over 20 years; Öcalan's "misilleme" claim rests on Sakık's book; the "they made us kill" line is a lawyer's relay.
- **Suspicion flag:** (a) **strong** — Öcalan claimed → denied → reframed as a state provocation. (b) — Öcalan's own "bize öldürttüler" is read by MİT-theory proponents as an admission of being *used*, and by the PKK as proof of state provocation (a Sakık-as-agent theory). Both readings are speculative.

### 17. 28 August 1998 — Third unilateral ceasefire announced by phone to a MED TV press conference in Brussels (effective 1 Sept 1998, World Peace Day)
- **Context:** ~25 mainstream Turkish journalists (incl. Tayfun Talipoğlu) took part live for the first time; Öcalan spoke from Damascus.
- **Quotes:**
  - "Tek taraflı demeyeceğim veya tek taraflı gibi de anlaşılsa bir ateşkesi başlatmayı uygun bulmaktayız." — *"I won't call it unilateral, or even if it is understood as unilateral, we find it appropriate to start a ceasefire."* (**V**)
  - "Türk askerinin bölgedeki hükümranlığını tartışmıyoruz." — *"We are not disputing the sovereignty of the Turkish army in the region."* (**V**)
  - "İlke olarak Cumhuriyet'e karşı çıkmıyoruz." — *"As a matter of principle we are not opposing the Republic."* (**V**)
- **Sources:** https://ilketv.com.tr/1998-ateskesine-giden-surec-ocalan-ile-aracili-ve-dolayli-gorusmeler/ ; https://www.ozgurpolitika.com/haberi-ciyaye-metina-savasi-ocalanin-basin-aciklamasi-113781 ; https://serbestiyet.com/featured/bir-roportajin-uzun-tarihi-222392/
- **Reliability:** Medium-high (consistent across pro-PKK and mainstream retellings).
- **Suspicion flag:** (b) — "warm messages to the army" six weeks before Syria expelled him are read by MİT-theorists as pre-negotiated; more plausibly they reflect Syrian pressure (Turkey's Oct 1998 ultimatum was already building).

### 23. 15/16 February 1999 — Captured at Nairobi airport; **statements on the plane** (video released by Turkish state, aired 17–18 Feb 1999)
- **Quotes (Turkish, from published transcript — V, but hostile-source transcript; re-verify on video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z1aEnH1LNCQ):**
  - "Ben ülkemi severim. Annem de Türk'tü." — *"I love my country. My mother was Turkish too."*
  - "Bir hizmet imkânım olursa yaparım. Onun dışında bana bir şey söylemeyin. Hizmet gerekirse yaparım." — *"If I have a chance to serve, I will. Beyond that don't tell me anything. If service is needed, I'll do it."*
  - Official: "Sorulara cevap verirsen, hizmet yapmış olursun."
  - "Türkiye'ye dönünce hizmet edeceğim. Fırsat verirseniz, hizmet ederim. Bunları halkın içinde konuşuyorum. Başka bir şey de konuşmam."
  - "Bir hizmet imkânım varsa, ben inanıyorum vardır, daha üst düzeydekilere de bildirirsek, ben hizmeti seve seve ederim. Ben hizmet edeceğim. Çok iyi edeceğim."
  - Official: "Şimdi bak kaydediyoruz, senin şeylerini." — Öcalan: "Yayınlayın."
  - "İşkence etmediniz, benim içimden geliyor. Ama ben gerçekten söylüyorum. Türkiye'yi seviyorum. Ve Türk halkını da seviyorum. Onlar için iyi hizmet edeceğime inanıyorum. Fırsat verilirse yaparım." — *"You didn't torture me, this comes from within. I'm really saying it: I love Turkey, and I love the Turkish people too…"*

- **Suspicion flag:** (a) **strong** vs. 20 years of "Kürdistan" rhetoric; (b) **the single most-cited item** in the "MİT's man" narrative (Kurdish nationalist critics, e.g. Arif Zêrevan: "Türk devletinin Kürtler içindeki bir numaralı sağlam adamı", https://x.com/ArifZerevan/status/1931715368097055196). **Overall assessment (skeptical):** For 1990–99 the evidentiary base for "Öcalan = MİT agent" consists of (1) a defector's evolving testimony partly proven forged by the General Staff, (2) the family background of an ex-wife whom Öcalan himself purged as a suspected agent, (3) a dead 1978-era courier whom Öcalan says he knowingly used, (4) one journalist's memory of a 1966 office boy, and (5) Öcalan's post-capture statements made hooded on a plane and in a death-penalty trial. Against this stand: the state's sustained war, the expulsion from Syria under threat of invasion, the CIA-assisted abduction, the death sentence, and the fact that the *andıç* proves the security apparatus manufactured "PKK-link" evidence in exactly this period. What the record *does* show is a leader who continuously sought and publicised state channels (Özal 1993, Erbakan/Yılmaz 1996, MED TV 1998) and who reversed his programme within weeks of capture — fertile ground for the theory, not proof of it.
