# Part 5 — The "Öcalan was/is a MİT agent / PKK is a state project" thesis: evidence dossier

**Compiled:** 2026-09-06
**Analyst stance:** skeptical OSINT; every claim tested for origin, chain of custody, and falsifiability.

## 0. Method and source-access caveat (read first)

- Sources were located via web search in Turkish, Kurdish (Kurmanci) and English. **Full-text retrieval of nearly every source was blocked by the session's egress proxy** (t24, Hürriyet, Milliyet, NTV, Sabah, Cumhuriyet, bianet, Wikipedia/Wikisource, umag.org.tr, dergipark, academia.edu, time.com, Rudaw, Basnews, K24, etc. all returned `EGRESS_BLOCKED`). The web-search budget was also exhausted mid-task.
- Consequently, quotations below are taken **only from search-engine result snippets** (which reproduce text from the linked page) or are flagged as **[background knowledge – not re-verified in this session]**. Nothing is invented; where I could not confirm wording, I say so.
- Quote-tags used: **[VERIFIED-SNIPPET]** = wording seen in a search snippet from the linked URL; **[UNVERIFIED]** = widely circulated but not confirmed against a primary text here; **[BACKGROUND]** = analyst's prior knowledge, needs citation before external use.
- Kurdish-language searches ("Ocalan ajanê MÎT", "PKK projeya dewletê", Rudaw/Basnews/K24 kurmancî) returned **no Kurdish-language articles advancing the agent thesis** in the result sets; the Kurmanci results were about the 2025 disarmament process. The thesis appears to live overwhelmingly in Turkish-language (and some diaspora Kurdish/PSK-aligned) discourse. This is itself a finding (see §17).

## 1. The 1972 Mamak arrest and "quick" release

**Claim.** Öcalan was arrested in April 1972 (Şafak/TİİKP leaflet distribution, Ankara SBF), held ~7 months in Mamak military prison, and released unusually early; critics infer that MİT recruited him in Mamak, or that the release itself shows protection.

**Origin.** Raised in the 1990s by Uğur Mumcu in *Kürt Dosyası* (1993) as a factual timeline; turned into an accusation by Turkish-nationalist / Islamist commentators (e.g., Abdurrahman Dilipak, 2019: "Öcalan 1972'den beri MİT elemanı") and by Mehmet Ali Güller ("MİT'ten MİT'e Öcalan", 2013).

**Verbatim / near-verbatim evidence.**
- Timeline per search snippets of the 1999 Ankara DGM reasoned judgment and biographies: taken into custody 7 April 1972, formally arrested 27 April, released 24 October 1972 "after six and a half months in detention"; one source says released "due to lack of evidence". [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://tr.wikisource.org/wiki/%C3%96calan_davas%C4%B1_gerek%C3%A7eli_karar%C4%B1 ; https://www.biyografya.com/tr/biographies/abdullah-ocalan-5a9e3ad5
- Dilipak (Akit, Nov 2019): claimed Öcalan "1972 yılından beri MİT elemanı" and that "birçok devlet yetkilisi ve üst düzey isim" knew; also referenced Baki Tuğ and Bahçeli as people who "could not not know". [VERIFIED-SNIPPET, paraphrase of snippet] — https://www.kurdistan24.net/tr/story/446934 ; https://anlatilaninotesi.com.tr/20191103/dilipakin-iddiasi-ocalan-1972den-beri-mit-elemani-bahceli-baki-tug-bilmiyor-olamaz-1040543470.html
- A snippet (mepanews) states: "1972'de Öcalan gözaltına alındı ve MİT'in müdahale ettiği iddialarıyla 3 aylık hafif bir cezayla serbest bırakıldı" — note the internal inconsistency (3 months vs 7 months). [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://www.mepanews.com/dilipak-ocalan-1972den-bu-yana-mit-elemani-30868h.htm
- Retired military judge **Baki Tuğ** (who prosecuted 1971-72 leftists in Ankara) is cited as suspecting that Pilot Necati Kaya was "a CIA/MİT operative who financed both THKP-C leader Mahir Çayan and Öcalan". [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://www.medyafaresi.com/haber/abdullah-ocalanin-kara-kutusu-kim-bu-pilot-necati/61936

**Öcalan's own account.** [BACKGROUND] In his defences and in Mahir Sayın's 1996 interview-book *Erkeği Öldürmek* he describes Mamak as where he "became a revolutionary", and describes hunger strikes; he does not describe recruitment. No primary text was retrievable in this session.

**Supporting evidence for the thesis.** (a) Release without conviction while co-defendants faced heavy demands; (b) his later trajectory: from a low-profile SBF student to founder of the most durable armed movement.

**Contradicting evidence.** (a) Mass releases of 1971-72 detainees in late 1972 – early 1973 were routine; hundreds of Şafak/TİİKP detainees were released pending trial, and the whole TİİKP case ended in the 1974 amnesty. Öcalan was a minor figure (a leaflet distributor), not a leader, so a release on lack of evidence is unremarkable. [BACKGROUND] (b) The DGM judgment itself, which had every incentive to expose a "MİT past", records only the ordinary detention/release facts. (c) Dilipak's claim is unsourced and contains a factual error (3 months).

**Assessment: Weak.** The only concrete fact (a 6.5-month detention ending in release without conviction) is ordinary for the period. Inference to recruitment is speculative and no document, witness or official has ever been produced.

## 2. "Pilot" Necati Kaya

**Claim.** Necati Kaya ("Pilot Necati", b. Ağrı 1956 per one snippet; other sources give an older birth date — inconsistent), an agricultural pilot, hosted the PKK's first formal 3-day meeting in Ankara (Jan 1977), gave money, flew Öcalan and Kesire to Diyarbakır after their 1978 wedding, and was a MİT agent placed on Öcalan. Öcalan called him "can simidi" (life-preserver) and admitted knowing him. Kaya died in a 1982 crop-duster crash near Manavgat under suspicious circumstances.

**Origin.** The earliest public accusation is a **2 September 1979 statement by a PKK splinter group published in *Aydınlık***, which said members had warned Öcalan that the "Pilot" was a MİT agent and provided documentation; Öcalan reportedly answered that the man was his "life preserver". Uğur Mumcu's column "Kim Bu Pilot?.." and *Kürt Dosyası* (1993) made the issue mainstream; Necdet Pekmezci's book *PKK'yı Kim Kurdu: Derin Devletin Gizemli Adamı Pilot Necati* (2010s) and Aziz Üstel's Star series are the main later compilations.

**Verbatim evidence.**
- "Öcalan reportedly referred to Pilot Necati as 'O benim cankurtaran simidimdir'." [VERIFIED-SNIPPET, indirect] — https://www.nesra.org/abdullah-ocalan-ve-pkk-hakkinda-kamuoyundaki-supheler/
- Öcalan to his lawyers (İmralı, 2000s, per Milliyet/T24/Vatan, 2011): "Pilot Necati'nin bizimle olduğu bir yıl çok önemliydi… Ajan olup olmadığı belirsiz, ama eğer ajansa durum değişir" (English rendering in snippet: "Whether he was an agent or not is uncertain, but if he was, the situation changes"). He also said Necati proposed "let's kidnap Sabiha Gökçen", and asked that Necati's death "and who killed him" be investigated. [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://www.milliyet.com.tr/gundem/iste-ocalanin-can-simidi-pilot-necati-1376058 ; https://t24.com.tr/gundem/aponun-can-simidi-mitci-pilot-necati,138224
- "The Aydınlık newspaper, which began daily publication in 1978, was the first to raise the issue of Pilot Necati and write that he was a person with dark relationships." [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://www.nesra.org/... (above)
- "Haki Karer … sent a letter to colleagues in Ankara examining Abdullah Öcalan-Pilot and Abdullah-Kesire relationships." Haki Karer was killed in Antep in May 1977. [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://www.nesra.org/... ; https://www.aydinlik.com.tr/koseyazisi/haki-karer-cinayeti-32344
- "Mehmet Turan, who knew Öcalan's relationship with MİT agent Pilot Necati in detail, was killed in Mardin in 1979." [VERIFIED-SNIPPET, allegation by the source] — https://www.nesra.org/...
- Ergenekon-related: "according to a 2000 document obtained during the Ergenekon operation, Pilot Necati provided 10 million lira to Öcalan for PKK's founding." [VERIFIED-SNIPPET; document itself not seen] — https://www.star.com.tr/yazar/abdullah-ocalan-dosyasiiii-pilot-necati-ve-abdullah-ocalan-yazi-1072326/
- Öcalan in *Erkeği Öldürmek* (1996, Mahir Sayın): money came "from his family and from Pilot Necati's salary, which they spent completely… 'not a single comrade failed to eat from it'"; and the line "örtülü ödenekten bunun için paralar sonuna kadar gözden çıkarılır". [VERIFIED-SNIPPET; wording as reported by T24's 2009 Ergenekon-indictment coverage; context disputed] — https://t24.com.tr/gundem/pkk-devletten-alinan-parayla-kurulmus,15197
- Crash: "Pilot Necati Kaya's crop-dusting aircraft crashed near Niğip village, Manavgat, after hitting high-voltage lines, 1982… according to eyewitnesses in a book, Kaya survived the crash with light injuries" (i.e. he may have died later/otherwise). [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://www.medyafaresi.com/haber/abdullah-ocalanin-kara-kutusu-kim-bu-pilot-necati/61936
- Öcalan's oft-quoted line "MİT bizi kullanmak istedi, biz de onları kullandık" ("MİT wanted to use us, and we also used them") appears in multiple snippets (Hürriyet 2003 "Öcalan MİT'te ofis-boy'du", Nesra, eksisözlük). Attribution chain: reported as said to Yalçın Küçük / in lawyer notes. **Primary source not located; treat as [UNVERIFIED] wording.** — https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/gundem/ocalan-mitte-ofis-boydu-178504

**When did Öcalan first admit knowing him?** Not "admitted" as a late confession: the 1979 Aydınlık text already shows Öcalan defending him inside the group; in *Erkeği Öldürmek* (1996) he speaks openly of Pilot's salary; in İmralı lawyer meetings (c. 2005-2011) he calls the year with Pilot "very important" and says it is "unclear" whether he was an agent. So the relationship was never denied; what he has never conceded is that *he* worked for MİT.

**Supporting evidence.** Contemporaneous (1979) internal accusation with claimed documents; Öcalan's own acknowledgment of financial dependence; the meeting held at Kaya's home; the pattern that the two people who pushed the issue (Haki Karer, Mehmet Turan) died violently; Kaya's own unexplained death.

**Contradicting evidence / weaknesses.** (a) No MİT personnel record, payslip or handler statement for Kaya has ever surfaced; the "documents" the 1979 group said they held were never published. (b) Aydınlık/TİİKP (Perinçek) was at that time in a bitter rivalry with Apocular and is not a neutral witness; Perinçek later personally maintained "PKK'yı MİT kurdu" through the 1990s while simultaneously being photographed with Öcalan in Bekaa (per Ergenekon indictment). (c) That MİT ran an informant near Öcalan (probable) is different from Öcalan being MİT's man; every 1970s left group had informants. (d) Öcalan's willingness to name and question Pilot is the opposite of what an agent protecting his handler would do — though critics argue this is limited hang-out.

**Assessment: Plausible that Necati Kaya was a MİT informant/asset; Weak as evidence that Öcalan was an agent.** The strongest single strand of the whole thesis, but it establishes proximity/penetration, not control.

## 3. Kesire Yıldırım and her father Ali Yıldırım

**Claim.** Kesire (b. 1951, Karakoçan; Alevi Kurd), the PKK's first female member, married Öcalan in Ankara on 24 May 1978; her father Ali Yıldırım had worked for MAH/MİT (Turkey's pre-1965 intelligence service) and allegedly reported on Öcalan; the marriage was therefore a state operation; Kesire split in 1988 and was declared a traitor.

**Origin.** Internal PKK suspicion from 1977 (Haki Karer letter); Mumcu 1993; became a media event on 18 Oct 2011 when a photo of Ali Yıldırım "emerged" (Sabah, A Haber, NTV, Habertürk) with the anecdote that he introduced Öcalan at the MİT building as "Bizim Kesire'nin nişanlısı".

**Verbatim evidence.**
- "Ali Yıldırım worked at the Milli Emniyet Hizmetleri Riyaseti (MAH)… Although Ali Yıldırım severed his connections with MİT in the 1970s, he is alleged to have provided information about Öcalan to the intelligence agency." [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kesire_Y%C4%B1ld%C4%B1r%C4%B1m
- Öcalan on Kesire: described her as "highly educated" but "I couldn't figure out whether she was an agent" ("ajan olup olmadığını çözemedim"). [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://www.ntv.com.tr/turkiye/ocalanin-mitci-kayinpederi,S3BpWXl1MEiKdFWegZcraA
- Öcalan (defence text, per snippet summarising a passage): denies Kesire was an agent "and suggest[s] that such claims stem from her father's background, with references to the Independence Court files" — i.e. Öcalan frames Ali Yıldırım as a Dersim-1938-era figure whose MAH link was known and old. [VERIFIED-SNIPPET, paraphrase] — search snippet for "Sümer Rahip Devletinden…" / *Bir Halkı Savunmak*
- Öcalan: "Kesire kaçtığı gün… yeniden doğdum" (felt liberated / "was reborn" the day Kesire fled). [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://issuu.com/hayatibalikoglu/docs/bir_halk___savunmak-abdullah_ocalan
- "Yıldırım: Kesire, Öcalan'ın gizli bağlantılarını öğrenince ayrıldı" — a 2011 Etkihaber headline attributing to a relative (reported as "Yıldırım") the claim that Kesire left after learning of Öcalan's secret links. [VERIFIED-SNIPPET, headline only] — https://www.etkihaber.com/yildirim-kesire,-ocalanin-gizli-baglantilarini-ogrenince-ayrildi-2--41119h.htm
- Sabah 18 Oct 2011: Ali Yıldırım allegedly introduced Öcalan inside the MİT building as "Bizim Kesire'nin nişanlısı". [VERIFIED-SNIPPET headline; the witness is not identified in the snippet] — https://www.sabah.com.tr/gundem/2011/10/18/kayinpederi-mit-binasinda-ocalani-boyle-tanitmis
- Kesire in the Netherlands "has not spoken about the PKK or Öcalan for 22 years". [VERIFIED-SNIPPET]

**Did Öcalan call the marriage a "state plot"?** In his writings Öcalan repeatedly characterises the marriage as a trap/"komplo" and Kesire as part of a plan to control or paralyse him, and elsewhere attributes the plot to "the state" and to "Turkish and Kurdish primitive nationalism" combined. **The exact phrase "devlet komplosu" was not confirmed in this session** [BACKGROUND/UNVERIFIED]. What is confirmed is that he both (i) suspected her, (ii) formally denied she was an agent in a defence, and (iii) said he never resolved the question.

**Supporting evidence.** Father's MAH employment is uncontested; the 2011 photo; Öcalan's own ambivalence; the PKK's 1988 branding of Kesire and Baki Karer's group as "traitors and collaborators".

**Contradicting evidence.** (a) MAH employment in the 1950s-60s by an Alevi Kurdish civil servant from Karakoçan is a career fact, not a control channel; the Wikipedia summary itself says he "severed connections in the 1970s". (b) Kesire was a founding cadre for 10 years, attended the 1982 congress, and has been silent in exile for decades rather than "outed" by anyone; a state asset would have been a prime witness at the 1999 trial or for post-1999 propaganda — she was never produced. (c) If the marriage was a state plot, the target was Öcalan — which is the *opposite* of Öcalan being the state's agent. Many proponents do not notice that claims 2-3 argue for penetration *against* Öcalan, not for his recruitment.

**Assessment: Plausible (state penetration attempt); Weak-to-Unsupported (Öcalan as agent).**

## 4. The July 1979 exit to Syria "weeks before the coup"

**Claim.** Öcalan crossed from Suruç into Syria in July 1979 having been tipped off (by Pilot Necati / MİT) about the impending 12 September 1980 coup.

**Origin.** Circulates in nationalist columns (Güller 2013; Nesra; Aydınlık's Bayram Yurtçiçek series 2010s). One snippet states baldly: "Öcalan fled to Syria in 1979 with the support of elements within MİT." [VERIFIED-SNIPPET, unsourced claim] — https://mehmetaliguller.com/2013/04/06/mitten-mite-ocalan/

**Facts.** "Temmuz 1979'da… yaya olarak Suruç'tan Suriye'ye gitti"; he hid in Viranşehir/Suruç first; on 30 July 1979 the Hilvan Kurtbaşı attack took place. [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/PKK ; https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/haber/teroristbasi-ocalanin-suriye-gunleri-279846 ; https://medyascope.tv/2024/12/11/ocalanin-suriye-yillari-suriye-kurtlerini-nasil-etkiledi/
- The coup came **14 months** later (Sept 1980), not "weeks". The framing in the tasking is itself a distortion found in the propaganda.

**Contradicting evidence.** (a) Martial law was already in force in the south-east from Dec 1978 (post-Maraş); the Hilvan-Siverek feud war and the Bucak clan (Mehmet Celal Bucak attack, 30 July 1979) put a price on Öcalan; leaving the country was the obvious move without any tip-off. (b) Most PKK cadres who stayed were arrested (Diyarbakır Prison, 1980-84); Öcalan took only a handful with him — a state-run exfiltration would have looked different. (c) Several other Kurdish/left leaders also left in 1979-80 (Burkay himself left in 1980). [BACKGROUND]

**Assessment: Unsupported.** No source names the tipper, the date or the mechanism; the chronology ("weeks before") is wrong.

## 5. MİT reports and Mehmet Eymür's statements

**Claim.** Ankara Emniyet / MİT "PKK raporları" of the 1980s and the ex-MİT counter-terror chief Mehmet Eymür (d. 2024) confirm PKK-state links.

**What Eymür actually said (as far as retrievable).**
- Eymür claimed "Abdullah Çatlı worked with PKK members" and that during his time at MİT he "conducted various operations to capture Öcalan… using 'Yeşil' (Mahmut Yıldırım)". [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://www.basnews.com/tr/babat/722000
- Eymür's career: Ankara MİT follow-up branch chief 1975; Bulgaria 1980-82; author of the 1987 "MİT Raporu" on state-mafia links (Üruğ, Ağar); resigned 1988; later Counter-Terror chief (1990s), Susurluk-era whistleblower. [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://serbestiyet.com/haberler/portre-mehmet-eymurun-uc-donemi-154626/
- Eymür to Yalçın Küçük (on whether Küçük had MİT links): "he had no such information… 'We were sometimes monitoring your activities abroad based on the information provided to us'." [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/haber/kucuke-ocalanin-beyni-misiniz-sorusu-81690
- [BACKGROUND, unverified this session] In interviews Eymür consistently framed Öcalan as a target, described the failed 1990s assassination/capture plans (Çiller's order to bring him "in a cage"), and did **not** claim Öcalan was an asset; his "third period" (2010s, blog "atin.org") contained accusations that MİT under Fidan was "negotiating with terrorists", not that Öcalan was MİT's creation.

**Ankara Emniyet report.** No such document was retrievable; references in the tasking could not be corroborated. [UNVERIFIED]

**Assessment: Unsupported as pro-thesis evidence; in fact Eymür's statements are contra-evidence** (he describes years of operations to kill or capture Öcalan, including the use of Yeşil). The only pro-thesis reading is that MİT "kept Öcalan under control through people around him" — which is standard intelligence coverage.

## 6. Kemal Burkay: "PKK'yı MİT kurdu / derin devlet kurdu"

**Claim.** Burkay (PSK/TKSP leader, 1937-, in exile 1980-2011) says the PKK was created in 1977 by the deep state, that MİT supervised its founding, and that after 1980 it passed to Syrian control; that Öcalan was "run" by Ergenekon.

**Verbatim.**
- "Öcalan, PKK'yı kurarken içinde bulunduğu ilişkiler hem Türkler hem de Kürtler için skandaldır, trajikomiktir. MİT, kuruluş aşamasında örgütü denetledi. Öcalan, PKK'yı MİT'in güdümünde kurdu." [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://www.aydinlik.com.tr/haber/kemal-burkay-pkkyi-mit-kurdurdu-195713
- "PKK, bir devlet projesidir"; PKK "1977 yılında derin devlet tarafından kurulduğunu, 1980'den sonra ise Suriye'nin kontrolüne girdiğini". [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://t24.com.tr/haber/kemal-burkay-pkkyi-derin-devlet-kurdu-apoyu-ergenekon-yonetti,192484
- "PKK'nın, Kürt yurtsever örgütlerine karşı savaşmak, onları ortadan kaldırmak için bizzat sömürgeci rejim tarafından kurulduğunu" (from his older writings, quoted by Madiya "PKK'yı kimler kurdu?"). [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://madiya.net/dizi-yazilar/pkk-yi-kimler-kurdu/

**His evidence.** In every retrievable statement Burkay's evidence is: Pilot Necati, Kesire/Ali Yıldırım, the PKK's 1978-80 attacks on rival Kurdish groups (KUK, TKSP, Halkın Kurtuluşu, DDKD) and on Kurdish landlords rather than the state, the "ease" of the 1979 exit, and the 1999 conduct. He cites **no document and no witness of his own**.

**Political incentive.** The PKK physically attacked and killed PSK/TKSP members in 1978-80 and again in the diaspora; Burkay's organisation was destroyed as a mass force by PKK violence. His return to Turkey in 2011 was welcomed by AKP media and used against the BDP. He is a hostile witness with a strong motive; this does not make him wrong but it lowers weight.

**Contradicting evidence.** Burkay himself concedes Syrian control after 1980, which sits awkwardly with continuous MİT control; his thesis implies MİT created an organisation that then killed ~6,000+ Turkish soldiers/police and destroyed the Turkish left in the region — a self-inflicted wound with no plausible cost-benefit.

**Assessment: Weak.** Opinion of a rival leader, derived entirely from claims 2-4, unsupported by independent evidence.

## 7. Şemdin Sakık (1998) and the "andıç"

**Claim.** After his capture (13 April 1998, Duhok, by Turkish special forces), PKK ex-commander Sakık "confessed" that Öcalan was a MİT agent and named journalists and civil-society figures as PKK collaborators; Hürriyet ("Dehşet İtiraflar", 25 April 1998) and Sabah ran the material.

**What happened.**
- "In 1998 … a plan was prepared by the General Staff under the orders of generals Çevik Bir and Erol Özkasnak, under which false statements would be added to Sakık's interrogation documents and then leaked to the press… Hürriyet's headline on April 25, 1998 was 'Dehşet İtiraflar'." [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://t24.com.tr/medya/mehmet-altan-yazdi-bir-andic-skandali,1030668 ; https://www.ihd.org.tr/nisan-1998-tarihli-andic-belgesi-hakkinda-suc-duyurusu/
- "Sakık's statement did not contain these accusations, and it was understood that the statements in question were written down by soldiers." [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://bianet.org/haber/ergenekon-davasi-nin-gizli-tanigi-semdin-sakik-141878
- At Diyarbakır DGM Sakık "rejected the reports that appeared in the media… revealing that these reports were sent to the media intentionally and for criminal purposes by the suspicious official and his team". [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — same
- Sakık later became a secret witness in Ergenekon (2011-12) and wrote a book *Apo* (2012) portraying Öcalan as a ruthless opportunist; his framing there is that Öcalan was **used** by the deep state and "Ergenekon", not that he held a MİT card. [VERIFIED-SNIPPET for existence of book; content characterisation BACKGROUND] — https://www.haber7.com/siyaset/haber/81609-semdin-sakiktan-apo-kitabi

**Assessment: Refuted as originally published.** The 1998 "Apo is MİT" confession was a psychological-operations product of the General Staff (the 1998 andıç), documented in later court files; Sakık's own later claims are those of a hostile captive/witness and are unverifiable.

## 8. Hüseyin Kutlu / Nurettin Yılmaz / "Terzi Cemal"

- **Nurettin Yılmaz** (ex-MP; Mardin/Diyarbakır region, Bucak-adjacent circles) is periodically cited for the claim that Öcalan met MİT officers in Diyarbakır in 1978-79. **No retrievable source in this session**; [UNVERIFIED].
- **Hüseyin Kutlu**: searches returned nothing attributable to a person of this name on this topic; the results redirected to the Avni Özgürel "Fikir Ajansı" anecdote (Öcalan as an "office boy" at a MİT-linked front called Fikir Ajansı in early-1970s Ankara). Özgürel: the place "was not MİT headquarters but a subsidiary organisation called 'Fikir Ajansı' established by MİT to support nationalist circles". [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/gundem/ocalan-mitte-ofis-boydu-178504. This single-witness anecdote (Özgürel was then a nationalist student journalist) is the concrete kernel of the "Öcalan worked at MİT" meme. Not corroborated by anyone else; Öcalan's known 1969-70 Ülkü Ocakları/Islamist-sympathy period at Tapu-Kadastro school in Ankara is well documented and makes a low-level job at a nationalist front conceivable without implying recruitment.
- **"Terzi Cemal"** (Cemal Şerik): search returned nothing. [UNVERIFIED — not found].

**Assessment: Unsupported** (Kutlu, Yılmaz, Terzi Cemal); **Weak** (Özgürel anecdote — single uncorroborated witness, and even if true describes an errand-boy job at a nationalist think-tank front, not intelligence employment).

## 9. Ali Kemal Özcan and Yalçın Küçük

**Ali Kemal Özcan** (Munzur Univ.; author of *Turkey's Kurds: A Theoretical Analysis of the PKK and Abdullah Öcalan*, Routledge 2006). He is not a proponent of the agent thesis; on the contrary he treats Öcalan as the state's only real interlocutor. Retrievable statements: he met Öcalan on İmralı "several times with state officials' approval", "a state official was also present", "Bana devlet haber verdi"; he described an "Erdoğansız-Öcalansız çözüm" project that Bahçeli "disrupted". ## 10. The 1999 capture and Öcalan's first statements

**Claim.** The Nairobi capture (15 Feb 1999) was a negotiated handover in which Öcalan was "delivered" (with CIA/Mossad help) rather than captured, and his immediate statements of loyalty ("Annem Türk", "hizmete hazırım") prove he was the state's man all along.

**Facts.** Captured "by Turkish special agents acting in connection with CIA and Mossad, while en route from the Greek embassy to Nairobi airport"; "CIA operatives tracked Öcalan to the Greek ambassador's residence"; Kenyan authorities "under huge pressure… handed him over"; the White House denied "direct involvement in Öcalan's handover to Turkey". [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://time.com/archive/6910967/behind-ocalans-capture-deceit-abduction-and-the-mossad/ ; https://www.wrmea.org/1999-april-may/capture-of-kurdish-rebel-leader-ocalan-recalls-mossad-collaboration-with-both-turkey-kurds.html ; https://medyanews.net/abdullah-ocalans-1998-odyssey-unravelling-an-international-conspiracy/

**First statements.** On the plane a Turkish official said "Abdullah Öcalan, memlekete hoş geldin"; Öcalan replied: "Ben ülkemi severim. Annem de Türk'tü. Bir hizmet imkânım olursa yaparım." [VERIFIED-SNIPPET] — https://www.yenisafak.com/gundem/aponun-ucaktaki-ilk-ifadeleri-hizmete-hazirim-172524 ; Fehmi Çalmuk (TV100, Oct 2024) recalled the words as "Benim Annem Türk'tür. Eğer Türkiye Cumhuriyeti Devleti bana bir görev verirse hazırım". — https://x.com/tv100/status/1848734211529970080. İmralı interrogation video (secretly recorded 1999) shows him "generally binding the Kurds to Turkey and saying he would now work for Turkey". — https://tr.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullah_%C3%96calan'%C4%B1n_sorgulanmas%C4%B1

**Pro-thesis reading.** The abrupt pivot from "independent Kurdistan" to "I'll serve the Republic" within hours, the state's decision not to execute him (2002 abolition), and the İmralı single-inmate regime are read as protecting an asset.

**Contra reading.** (a) The capture required a 4-month multinational hunt (Rome, Moscow, Minsk, Athens, Corfu, Nairobi), an Italy-Turkey diplomatic crisis, the fall of a Greek foreign minister, and US/Israeli intelligence assets. A "negotiated handover" of one's own asset does not require Mossad and a global manhunt. (b) The loyalty statements are consistent with a captured leader under drug-and-hood conditions bargaining for his life and his movement; the death sentence (June 1999) was real and only commuted because of EU accession politics and ECtHR interim measures (2002). (c) Öcalan's own defence frames 15 February as "devletlerarası komplo" — the exact opposite of a friendly handover — and the PKK's global mobilisation (self-immolations, consulate occupations) would be absurd theatre if the leadership knew otherwise. (d) The Kurdish-movement reading (CIA/Mossad conspiracy) and the Turkish-nationalist reading (state protects its agent) are mutually contradictory, yet the thesis uses both.

**Assessment: Refuted (negotiated handover); Weak (loyalty statements as proof).** The statements are real; the inference is not.

**Assessment: Unsupported (as proof of "handling"); Strong only as proof that the state regards Öcalan as the decisive interlocutor** — a fact that both readings accept.

**Assessment: Weak.** Establishes brutality and paranoia, not concealment of a state file.

## 16. Counter-arguments: scholarship, scale, state conduct, logic, incentives

**16.1 Scholarship.** None of the standard academic histories endorses the thesis:
- Aliza Marcus, *Blood and Belief* (NYU Press 2007): reconstructs the founding from ~100 interviews with ex-PKK cadres; treats Pilot Necati/Kesire suspicions as internal paranoia and Öcalan as a ruthless autonomous actor; notes the war "left nearly 40,000 people dead". [VERIFIED-SNIPPET for death toll; characterisation BACKGROUND] — https://nyupress.org/9780814757116/blood-and-belief/
- Ali Kemal Özcan, *Turkey's Kurds* (Routledge 2006); Cengiz Güneş, *The Kurdish National Movement in Turkey* (2012); Joost Jongerden & Ahmet Hamdi Akkaya, "Born from the Left: the making of the PKK" (2011) and "The PKK in the 2000s" — all trace the PKK to the 1970s Turkish revolutionary left (Dev-Genç, THKP-C legacy) and to Kurdish student circles in Ankara; none find a state genesis. Mesut Yeğen's work on the state's Kurdish discourse treats the "PKK is a foreign/state project" trope as part of official denial repertoire. [BACKGROUND — not re-verified]
- Nihat Ali Özcan (first Turkish PhD on the PKK, 1999; ex-officer, TEPAV) describes the PKK as a self-generated Marxist-Leninist organisation exploited by Syria/USSR-bloc, not a MİT creation. Search results did not surface a direct quote on the "MİT kurdu" claim. [VERIFIED-SNIPPET of his standing; position BACKGROUND] — https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/traddergi/article/1165678
- Turkish academic Ümit Yazıcıoğlu (2026): claims are "kanıtsız, bilimsel ve hukuki dayanaktan yoksun" (see §15).

**16.2 Scale of the war.** ~40,000 dead (Marcus; official Turkish figures similar), ~3,000-4,000 villages evacuated, >1 million displaced, 1990s state expenditure of several percent of GDP, ~17,000 unsolved murders attributed to the JİTEM era; PKK attacks killing thousands of soldiers, police and village guards; the 2015-16 urban war. A state does not run a 47-year insurgency against itself at that price; the thesis requires MİT to have lost control by ~1980 at latest — at which point it stops being a "MİT project" in any explanatory sense.

**16.3 State conduct toward Öcalan.** Multiple documented attempts to kill or seize him (1990s Çiller-era plans; Eymür/Yeşil operations; Syrian pressure Oct 1998 with threat of war; global manhunt 1998-99; death sentence 1999; solitary confinement since 1999; ban on lawyer visits for years). The claim that this was all theatre is unfalsifiable.

**16.4 Logical structure.** The thesis (a) explains both hostility and dialogue as "proof"; (b) treats Öcalan's own admissions of MİT proximity as confession, and his denials as cover; (c) merges two mutually exclusive stories (state asset vs. CIA/Mossad conspiracy victim); (d) rests on exactly two individuals (Pilot Necati, Kesire) from 1976-79 to explain 47 years. Every added contradiction is absorbed. That is the signature of a non-falsifiable narrative.

**16.5 Who spreads it and why.**
- Turkish nationalists / Kemalist "ulusalcı" (Perinçek/Aydınlık line, Güller, Yurtçiçek; later some MHP-adjacent and Islamist columnists like Dilipak): delegitimises the Kurdish movement as inauthentic while blaming the "deep state"/AKP/USA; since 2024 also used by nationalists opposing Bahçeli's opening.
- Rival Kurdish parties (Burkay/PSK, KUK-derived circles, some KDP-aligned media; Madiya): the PKK destroyed them in 1978-85 and monopolised Kurdish politics; "MİT kurdu" explains their defeat without conceding PKK popularity.
- PKK dissidents (Sakık, Baki Karer's HRK/"Devrimci Yol" splinters, Osman Öcalan circle): personal grievance and the need to explain their own decades in a "wrong" organisation.
- The AKP-aligned press (2011 Sabah/A Haber "MİT'çinin damadı" wave; 2009 Ergenekon-era T24/Taraf coverage) used it tactically against the "old deep state" and the BDP.
- The pro-PKK side has its own mirror-image conspiracy (Öcalan as victim of "uluslararası komplo", Gülenist/Israeli plots), which keeps the discourse polarised and evidence-free.

**Strongest pro-thesis dossier (steelman).** A young man with a nationalist/Islamist phase and a possible job at a MİT-front think-tank (Özgürel) is released early in 1972; his organisation's first congress is held in the flat of a man contemporaries documented as MİT (Aydınlık 1979) and financed by his salary — which Öcalan admits; he marries the daughter of a former MAH officer; the two cadres who investigated (Haki Karer, Mehmet Turan) die; he leaves Turkey before the coup while his cadres are jailed; his organisation destroys the rival Kurdish left; on capture he offers to "serve"; the state spares him, negotiates only through MİT, and in 2025 the head of the MHP thanks him by name. The thesis explains nothing that the "failed penetration + eventual negotiation" model does not explain more economically, and it can absorb any evidence, which is why scholarship classes it as conspiracy theory.
